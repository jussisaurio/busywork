# Level sketches: mechanic-first

Lens: one small reusable engine rule per level, built on level 1's systems. These mechanics are new.
Sketch 5 (phones) is close to the noise seed ("Weekend Shift"), but it is a *summon* (the NPC walks over and stays busy), not a glance.
Sketch 8 (robot vacuum) uses a moving object to force glances, so it can also share the noise code.

---

## Lights Out
**Mechanic:** Each room has a wall switch (interact 0.5 s, not sneaky, a loud *clack*). In a dark room, everyone's vision radius drops to 1.5 tiles. An NPC standing in the light cannot see into the dark beyond 1.5 tiles. An NPC standing in the dark sees lit areas normally. An NPC who enters a dark room, or whose own room goes dark, says 💡❓ and walks straight to its switch. The dark is drawn as a navy overlay. The player in the dark shows as a red outline with two eyes.
**Card:** 🔦 7:30 PM. Your resignation letter is in the boss's inbox. You've changed your mind.
**Setting & day:** Evening overtime. Boss, Lin, and Dolores the janitor 🧹.
- 6:40: Dolores turns off the kitchen light.
- 6:50: the boss walks to the copier.
- 7:00: Lin goes home and turns off the open plan.
- 7:15: Dolores empties the bins into the shredder.
- 7:30: the boss reads his inbox.
**Routes:**
1. Turn off the boss's office light while he sits. He walks to the switch by the door, and that is your window at the inbox. Trap: if you stand by the door, he walks into you.
2. Move the letter to his bin before 7:15, in the dark open plan (after 7:00), so Dolores shreds it. Trap: Dolores turns the lights on wherever she goes.
3. Turn off the copier corridor at 6:50. The boss can't find his way back, 😩🔦, and goes home.
**Lesson / fail:** You turn off a room while standing next to the switch. The NPC walks over and bumps into you: 💡 → ❗.
**Reuse:** Any night level, power-cut gags, and a heist finale.
**Funny because:** A grown man in a suit gropes along the wall for a switch with you standing right beside him.

## Office Time
**Mechanic:** NPCs follow the wall clock in their zone, not the true time. Each clock is an interact (sneaky, 1.5 s, you stand on a chair) that moves it +10 minutes. When an NPC looks up at a clock (🕐👀, on schedule beats and on sitting down), they jump to the schedule beat for the time it shows. The HUD clock and the deadline stay true. A clock you have moved shows slightly crooked hands.
**Card:** 🚌 Your bus leaves at 4:30. Nobody leaves before the boss.
**Setting & day:** Normal floor. One clock in the boss's office and one in the open plan.
- 3:00: Kev looks up and goes on his coffee run.
- 3:15: the boss returns from the restroom and sits (he looks up).
- 4:00: Lin packs up if the open-plan clock says 4:30 or later.
- 5:00: the boss leaves (🎒👋).
**Routes:**
1. Push the boss's clock +30 before he sits at 3:15. He leaves at 4:30 true time. Trap: that clock is behind his glass wall, in Lin's view.
2. Push the open-plan clock. Lin and Kev leave early. The boss sees two empty desks, 🤨, and leaves after them (the herd). Trap: Kev's coffee run jumps forward too, and it crosses your chair.
**Lesson / fail:** You push the clock +30. The boss looks up 🕐, jumps up, and starts his smoke break now, walking past your chair.
**Reuse:** Any schedule-driven level. Combines with every timed beat.
**Funny because:** You cheat time inside a time loop.

## The Monitor
**Mechanic:** A camera sweeps a grey cone (fixed period, like a seated glance). A monitor on the guard's desk shows a small live picture. An NPC watching the monitor has the camera's cone as extra vision, so the normal tattle and caught rules apply. Unplug the monitor (sneaky, behind the desk) and it shows static. The guard then says 📹❓ and walks to the camera. Push the camera with a broom (sneaky) and it faces the wall.
**Card:** 📦 Your embarrassing parcel is at the front desk. Lin collects the mail at 4:00.
**Setting & day:** Lobby, mailroom and a corridor. Guard Bob 💂.
- 2:45: Bob makes his rounds.
- 3:10: Bob eats at his desk (eyes on the monitor).
- 3:30: the boss passes the desk.
- 4:00: Lin collects the mail.
**Routes:**
1. Walk through the camera's blind arc during the sweep. Trap: the sweep is slower than it looks, and the corridor is on screen.
2. Unplug the monitor. Bob walks to the camera, which leaves the mail shelf behind his desk open. Trap: his walk back passes the shelf.
3. Turn the camera to the wall at 2:45 while Bob is away. Trap: on his 3:10 return he sees the wall on screen and walks over.
**Lesson / fail:** You sneak behind Bob. On the screen in front of him, a tiny red sweater appears. He points ❗👉 at the screen, and then at you.
**Reuse:** Banks, the boss's office, and the finale (you watch the monitor).
**Funny because:** You can see yourself on the monitor, mid-crime.

## Going Down
**Mechanic:** One elevator with a lamp above the doors that shows its floor. Callers wait, and the car arrives (ding). Inside, the car is a small room: anyone inside sees everyone inside, and closed doors block sight in and out. A button panel inside (interact) and a call button on each floor (interact, not sneaky) send the car. Pressing a button inside next to an NPC is sneaky. A prop in the doors (a held item) keeps the car on that floor.
**Card:** 🏢 4:00 PM. The CEO visits floor 4. Your desk is the messy one.
**Setting & day:** Floors 3 and 4, with the elevator between them. The CEO 🎩 and the boss.
- 3:20: the boss rides down to meet the CEO.
- 3:45: they ride up together.
- 3:50: Kev rides down with a box.
- 4:00: the tour starts.
**Routes:**
1. Call the car to 3 at 3:44, from your floor. They get off on the wrong floor and tour floor 3. Trap: you must be back on 4 for the attendance-style check.
2. Prop the doors on 4 with Kev's box. The CEO waits, then takes the stairs, 😤, and arrives late. Trap: Kev reclaims his box if he sees you.
3. Ride with them, phone pose. Press 3 during the CEO's glance at the lamp.
**Lesson / fail:** In the car, you press a button. The CEO is 1 tile away and sees it: ❗.
**Reuse:** Multi-floor levels, Kev's exit, and chases.
**Funny because:** Elevator silence, plus you.

## Hold Music
**Mechanic:** A wall switchboard has one button per desk, each showing that owner's portrait. Press one, and that desk phone rings 📞🔔 (in earshot). The owner walks to it and talks for 1 game minute facing the wall. If the owner is away, the nearest NPC in earshot answers. You can unplug a phone cord (sneaky). The schedule also has real calls.
**Card:** 📞 Lin's interview for your job is a phone call at 3:30. She must miss it.
**Setting & day:** Normal floor with a kitchen phone.
- 3:00: Lin goes to the kitchen.
- 3:15: Kev does his cable round (he plugs in loose cords).
- 3:30: the interviewer calls Lin's desk.
- 3:35: the boss patrols.
**Routes:**
1. Ring the kitchen at 3:29 while Lin is nearby. She is stuck there, and the boss answers her real call: 😠📞. Trap: ring too early and she's back in time.
2. Unplug her cord during her kitchen trip. Trap: Kev's 3:15 round fixes it, so unplug after 3:15, while she's still away.
3. Call her desk from yours at 3:29 and stay on the line (pretend). The interviewer gets busy 📵. Trap: Lin answers your call and hangs up 😒 after 20 s, so ring her when she's away.
**Lesson / fail:** You ring the kitchen. Lin walks past it, ignores it, and Kev answers.
**Reuse:** Any level that needs an NPC moved. A cheap "summon" lure.
**Funny because:** The boss politely tells the interviewer that Lin is "very motivated."

## Print Queue
**Mechanic:** The printer runs jobs one at a time. The queue shows above it as stacked owner icons. When a job finishes, its owner hears the whirr and walks over. Whoever stands at the tray takes **everything** on it and leafs through it 👀📄. Swapping the top two jobs at the printer is sneaky. A paper ream (held item, from the closet) refills the printer. When it is empty, everything stalls.
**Card:** 🖨️ You sent your "I quit" rant to the shared printer. It's fourth in line.
**Setting & day:** Queue: the boss (50 pages), Lin, you, Kev.
- 3:00: the boss's job starts.
- 3:12: paper runs low.
- 3:20: Lin's job, then yours right after.
- 3:40: Kev's job.
**Routes:**
1. Swap so yours prints before the boss's, while he walks in. Then stand at the tray first. Trap: he walks in at your shoulder.
2. Take the ream before 3:12. The printer runs dry, and your job never prints. Trap: the boss hunts the ream (owner logic), so keep it hidden.
3. Let Lin's and yours print together and be at the tray first. Trap: she hears the whirr too.
**Lesson / fail:** Lin picks up her stack, reads the top sheet, 😳, and takes it to the boss.
**Reuse:** Microwave, coffee machine, and restroom queues.
**Funny because:** The whole office crowds round one beige machine.

## Fire Door
**Mechanic:** Closed doors block sight. Self-closing doors shut 3 s after someone passes (thunk: people inside glance at the door). A keyed door latches and opens only with a beep, after Kev's badge tap. A held item placed in a doorway props it open (a new "wedge" interact). Dolores kicks away props and keeps them, 😠.
**Card:** 🗄️ Your fudged expenses are in the file room. The audit is at 4:00.
**Setting & day:** File room (keyed), a corridor with a fire door, and the kitchen.
- 3:00: the auditor goes in.
- 3:10: Kev fetches a server box.
- 3:25: Dolores sweeps the corridor.
- 3:40: the auditor takes coffee.
**Routes:**
1. Wedge your mug when the auditor enters. Trap: Dolores at 3:25.
2. Tailgate Kev. Trap: stand in the closing door and it thunks, so Kev turns.
3. Shut the kitchen door at 3:40. The auditor's sightline is cut, so you can work in the file room.
**Lesson / fail:** The door shuts behind you, thunk, and every head inside turns ❗.
**Reuse:** Any multi-room level. Doors and keys are a staple.
**Funny because:** The door gives you away, every time.

## Robo-Vac
**Mechanic:** A robot vacuum runs a fixed path at fixed times. It pushes small floor items ahead of it and carries an item set on top (interact) to its dock. NPCs within 3 tiles watch it go by for 1.5 s (a forced glance that moves with it). It goes under doors.
**Card:** 👞 Your shoe is in the boss's office. Don't ask.
**Setting & day:**
- 2:40: the vacuum crosses the open plan.
- 3:05: it goes through the boss's office.
- 3:30: it docks in the hallway.
- 3:50: the boss looks under his desk.
**Routes:**
1. Wait for the vacuum to nudge the shoe out to its dock. Trap: the boss's chair blocks the path unless he is away.
2. Walk in behind the vacuum while Lin watches it. Trap: the glance lasts only while it is within 3 tiles.
3. Put Lin's sandwich on top before 3:05. The vacuum carries it into the boss's office. Lin storms in after it 😠🥪, the boss turns to her, and you grab the shoe behind them. Trap: Lin sees you put it on, and she tattles.
**Lesson / fail:** The vacuum shoves your shoe right under the boss's gaze.
**Reuse:** Any level that needs a moving distraction or a courier.
**Funny because:** Everyone watches the robot, and so do you.

## Mechanics tally

| Mechanic | Size | How well it combines with level 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Lights / darkness | M | Strong. It changes vision radius, so glances and tattles still work, and the 💡❓ walk is a schedule override. |
| Wall clock (NPCs follow clocks) | M | Very strong. It reuses the whole schedule system. It needs beat re-entry for jumped NPCs. |
| CCTV monitor (remote vision) | M | Strong. It gives an extra cone to the watcher, so tattle and caught are reused unchanged. |
| Elevator | L | Medium. It needs multi-floor maps and pathing, but its sight rules are just walls. |
| Desk phones (summon) | S | Strong. It is a schedule interrupt plus a fixed pose. It shares the noise code. |
| Print queue | S–M | Medium. It reuses owners and held items (the ream). The queue UI is icons. |
| Self-closing / keyed doors | M | Strong. Doors are line-of-sight blockers, the thunk is a noise event, and the wedge is a new interact. |
| Robo-vac (moving object) | S–M | Strong. It is a scripted mover, pushing is simple physics, and the passing glance reuses the noise or glance code. |
