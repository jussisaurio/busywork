# Same office, new days: 8 level sketches

Same floor plan and cast (boss, Kev, Lin), with at most one new character per level. Times are departure times.
Several sketches share one generic mechanic, **inspection**: an NPC checks a fixed list of targets (an item on a spot, or a person in a place), shows ✔️/❌ over each, and a ❌ switches their schedule to a hunt or a stare.

## Fire Drill
**Card:** 🧯 Your typo-riddled report is on the boss's desk. Get it back.
**Day:** 2:44 boss patrols · 3:00 alarm 🔔, everyone files out · 3:06 roll call: the boss looks at each person 👓✔️ 🔧✔️ · 3:20 everyone back in · 3:40 the boss reads the report 📄😠 (fired).
**Routes:**
- Get counted, then slip back in behind Kev (warden, hi-vis 🦺). Trap: the boss faces the glass, so cross on his look-away.
- Skip the drill. The roll call shows 🧍❌ and Kev sweeps the floor east to west from 3:08. Trap: grab the report before he reaches the boss's office (~3:15).
- Take it during the 2:44 patrol and hold it to your ear at the roll call. Trap: the patrol passes his own door twice.
**Lesson:** being absent is visible. Fail: you stay at your desk, the lobby line shows a flashing gap 🧍❓, and Kev marches in.
**Engine:** schedules, patrol, glass, contraband, pretend · NEW: inspection · level hook: alarm/evacuation override
**Funny because:** a warden with a clipboard hunting one guy, at walking pace.

## Moustache Day
**Card:** 📸 3:45 team photo under the boss's portrait. Give him a moustache.
**Day:** 3:00 Kev sets up a tripod in the hallway facing the portrait (by the boss's door) · 3:12–3:22 boss in the restroom · 3:44 line-up, countdown beeps · 3:45 FLASH · 3:47 the boss checks the camera screen 👀📷.
**Routes:**
- Draw it at 3:15 while the boss is out. Trap: Lin can see the hallway, so draw on her glance away.
- Draw it during the countdown (every face on the lens), then reach your spot before the flash. Trap: in frame mid-scribble at the flash = on the screen at 3:47.
**Lesson:** a camera is a witness that tells on you later. Fail: the boss leans in to the screen, the photo zooms up on you mid-scribble, 😠👉.
**Engine:** schedules, glances, late tattle · NEW: snapshot witness (a fixed cone records sneaky acts at one moment; tattles when someone views it) · level hook: draw on the portrait
**Funny because:** the win screen is the official photo: proud boss, Sharpie moustache.

## Lights Out
**Card:** 🔌 Your angry resignation letter is in the boss's tray. Get it back.
**Day:** 3:10 Lin's microwave trips the fuse: dark, except near the glass entrance; everyone pulls out phone torches 📱 · 3:14 Kev walks a torch to the fuse box in the kitchen · 3:20 lights on · 3:40 the boss empties his tray 📄😠.
**Routes:**
- Grab it in the dark. Trap: the boss sweeps his torch around his office like a glance.
- Pull the breaker yourself at 3:13, while he walks to the restroom. Trap: Kev's torch comes down the hallway.
- Trip it again right after Kev fixes it. Trap: he's still standing at the fuse box.
**Lesson:** dark shrinks every view, but a torch is long and narrow. Fail: you stroll past Lin, sure you're invisible; her torch lands on your red sweater, ❗👉.
**Engine:** cones, glances, walks, tattle · NEW: lighting (light state scales view radius; a held torch is a narrow long cone) · level hook: fuse box
**Funny because:** a red sweater frozen mid-tiptoe in a torch beam.

## Surprise Party
**Card:** 🎈 Lin and Kev are decorating the kitchen. Keep the boss out of it until 3:30.
**Day:** 3:00 Lin and Kev hang balloons in the kitchen · 3:05 the boss walks to the kitchen for coffee · 3:12–3:22 restroom · 3:24 coffee walk again · 3:30 party 🎂🎉. Peeked: 😐🎈, glares at you.
**Routes:**
- Small talk: catch him in the hallway and chat 💬; he babbles awhile. Trap: one chat won't cover both walks.
- Pour the day's one cup before Kev's 2:38 and leave it on the boss's desk; he skips 3:05. Trap: Kev claims a visible cup.
- Cup for 3:05, chat for 3:24.
**Lesson:** you can be the distraction: talking to someone freezes their day. Fail: you stand in the kitchen doorway to block him; he walks straight through you.
**Engine:** schedules, owner takes back, held items · NEW: small talk (interact: both face each other, their schedule pauses N game minutes, then resumes late); put down item · level hook: none
**Funny because:** a man desperate for coffee, held hostage by weather chat.

## The Audit
**Card:** 🧾 3:30 laptop audit. There should be four. You sold yours.
**Day:** new: the **Auditor** 🕵️ (grey suit, clipboard) · 3:20 arrives by elevator · 3:25 boss's office · 3:30 Lin's desk · 3:35 your desk · 3:40 Kev's desk (each 💻✔️/❌) · 3:45 leaves. Any ❌: he shows the boss, 😠👉.
**Routes:**
- Once Lin's laptop is ticked, carry it to your desk before 3:35. Trap: she takes it back on sight, so hold it to your ear.
- Kev went home at 3:00 and left his. Desk-hop it to yours at 3:35, back by 3:40. Trap: a very tight walk.
- Borrow the boss's after his tick, while he smokes from 3:35. Trap: a long walk, with Lin watching through the glass.
**Lesson:** checks run in a fixed order, so one thing can count twice. Fail: you move Lin's laptop too early and her desk gets the ❌.
**Engine:** owners, held items, pretend, glances · NEW: put down item; inspection · level hook: none
**Funny because:** one laptop sprinting around, always a desk ahead of the clipboard.

## Kev's Hoodie
**Card:** 💻 The boss's screen shows your awful review. Delete it before he sends it.
**Day:** 3:12 boss to the restroom · 3:14 Kev, sweaty 🥵, leaves his hoodie on his chair and goes home (Lin sees him go; the boss doesn't) · 3:22 boss back · 3:30–3:37 Lin in the restroom · 3:45 SEND 📧.
**Routes:**
- In the hoodie, the boss sees "Kev" 🔧👍 and lets you "fix" his PC. Trap: Lin knows Kev left, so go while she's in the restroom.
- No hoodie: delete it during the boss's restroom trip. Trap: Lin faces his screen through the glass, so work on her glances.
**Lesson:** clothes are who you are, to people who don't know better. Fail: in the hoodie you pass Lin, who waved Kev off; she does a double take 🔧❓, ❗👉.
**Engine:** witnesses, tattle, glances · NEW: disguise (a worn item changes who NPCs think you are; someone who saw the real owner leave sees through it) · level hook: none
**Funny because:** the boss warmly thanking a red-sweatered "Kev", hood pulled tight.

## Late Again
**Card:** ⏰ 9:20 AM. Overslept, no keycard. Be at your desk by stand-up.
**Day:** the loop starts in the lobby, outside the glass · 9:22 Lin badges in with a latte (the door opens 2 s) · 9:30 Kev backs in with a monitor box that blocks his view · 9:40 the boss patrols · 9:50 stand-up: he checks each desk 🪑✔️/❌.
**Routes:**
- Tailgate Lin. Trap: she glances over her shoulder at the door.
- Tailgate Kev's box. Trap: the boss's office faces south over the floor, so folder-stride or be seen arriving.
- Catch a late door while the patrol is east. Trap: stand-up is close.
**Lesson:** doors open for other people, briefly. Fail: a step late, the door clunks shut in your face, and you bang on the glass 😠🚪🪪, just like the boss in level 1.
**Engine:** schedules, patrol, glances, folder stride · NEW: timed doors (open N s for a passing NPC; anyone may follow); inspection · level hook: spawn outside
**Funny because:** level 1's lockout gag, now aimed at you.

## Out by 3:30
**Card:** 🏖️ Your flight leaves at 4:30. Get out without being missed.
**Day:** 2:44 the boss patrols past your desk · 3:00 Kev takes the elevator home · 3:05, 3:25, 3:45 the boss looks over the desks from his glass wall (🪑✔️/❌) · 3:12–3:22 restroom · 3:35 smokes by the elevator.
**Routes:**
- Drape your jacket on your chair (a decoy: from afar, it's you) and ride down with Kev at 3:00. Trap: drape it before 2:44 and the patrol walks right past it 🧥❓.
- Decoy, then leave during the restroom gap. Trap: his walk back crosses your path.
- No decoy: slip out between the 3:05 and 3:25 looks. Trap: the slow elevator.
**Lesson:** a thing can stand in for you, from a distance. Fail: his 3:05 look lands on your empty chair 🪑❓, and he catches you at the elevator.
**Engine:** patrol, glass, elevator leave · NEW: put down item; inspection; decoy (an object passes a person check beyond N tiles) · level hook: leaving = win
**Funny because:** the boss nods approvingly at a jacket.

## Mechanics tally
- **Inspection** (an NPC checks a list of item-on-spot or person-in-place targets, ✔️/❌; a ❌ branches the schedule): **4** (Fire Drill, The Audit, Late Again, Out by 3:30)
- **Put down item on a spot**: **3** (Surprise Party, The Audit, Out by 3:30), plus the already-decided A1 mug ditch
- **Small talk** (chat to pause an NPC's schedule): **1** (Surprise Party)
- **Snapshot witness** (a fixed timed cone that records and tattles when viewed): **1** (Moustache Day)
- **Lighting** (dark scales view radius; torch cones): **1** (Lights Out)
- **Disguise** (a worn identity, broken by "saw the owner leave"): **1** (Kev's Hoodie)
- **Timed doors / tailgating**: **1** (Late Again)
- **Decoy** (an object passes a person check at range; builds on inspection and put down): **1** (Out by 3:30)

Build inspection and put down first: together they unlock 4 of these levels plus A1.
