# Level sketches: new workplaces

Same game, other jobs. Each sketch teaches one new thing on top of level 1.

## The Walk-In (restaurant kitchen)
**Card:** 🧅 8:00 PM. Chef wants forty kilos of onions peeled. Be nowhere until 9.
**Cast:**
- Chef 👨‍🍳: a tall toque, stands at the pass and rings the 🔔.
- Sous Marta 🔪: patrols the line.
- Server Jo 🍽️: goes through the swing door every ~6 min.
- Pip 🧽: the new dishwasher, gets whatever job nobody else takes.

**Day:**
- 8:00: Chef calls 📣🧅 and starts hunting you, like Lin's hunt.
- 8:12: Chef tastes at the stove, back to the room.
- 8:30: Marta fetches flour from the dry store.
- 8:40: Chef takes a delivery out back.
- 9:00: whoever Chef has found peels. Nobody found means Pip peels 😭🧅.

**Routes:**
1. **Walk-in freezer.** Hide inside. Stay more than ~8 min and you come out frosted 🥶, and Chef reads frost on sight. Thaw at the range while he's out back at 8:40.
2. **Dry store.** It's warm, but Marta walks in at 8:30 and there is no second exit. Leave before then.
3. **Shuttle.** Walk-in, then dry store, then walk-in again, slipping between them while Chef tastes at the stove (8:12) and when Jo's swing door blocks his view.

**Lesson:** hiding spots are safe but have a cost. The fail: you walk out of the walk-in, Chef sees the icicles 🥶👉, and you're on onions.
**Engine:** hunts, vision cones, evidence marks · NEW `hide spot` (a zone where you can't be seen, with a timer that leaves a mark) · NEW `door occludes` (an open swing door blocks sight) · level-only: none
**Funny because:** every cook has hidden in the walk-in, and it shows on your face.

## Handover (hospital ward)
**Card:** 📋 7:00 AM handover. You never filled in the obs charts. Make sure the consultant never reads them.
**Cast:**
- Matron 🩺: starched hat, walks a fixed loop of the beds and is the boss.
- Dr Hale 🥼: the consultant, arrives at 6:45. His white coat hangs on the hook at the ward door.
- Nurse Dee 💉: sits at the station and knows whose coat is whose.
- Mr Patel 🛏️: bed 3, presses his buzzer at 6:30.

**Day:**
- 6:30: Mr Patel's buzzer goes off, and Matron walks to bed 3 for 4 min.
- 6:40: Dee goes on her tea break.
- 6:45: Hale arrives and takes his coat.
- 6:55: the chart trolley is wheeled to bed 1.
- 7:00: handover rounds.

**Routes:**
1. **The coat.** Put on Hale's white coat. Matron doesn't watch doctors, so her ❓ stays still. Walk the charts to the shredder. Trap: Hale arrives at 6:45 and sees you in his coat 😠🥼.
2. **The buzzer.** Matron leaves for bed 3 at 6:30. Take the charts then, while Dee is facing the other way.
3. **The laundry.** Drop the charts into the laundry cart before the porter wheels it out at 6:50.

**Lesson:** a costume changes who watches you. The fail: in the coat you walk straight past Dee, who knows it's Hale's: ❗👉🥼.
**Engine:** suspicion, owners take back, tattles · NEW `disguise` (a worn item gives you a role; non-owners treat you as that role, the owner treats it as theft) · level-only: none
**Funny because:** everyone trusts a white coat, except the man it belongs to.

## Staff Room (school)
**Card:** 📝 3:30 bell. Thirty essays, unmarked. Make sure they come back marked.
**Cast:**
- Head 🎓: walks the corridor past the glass door and is the boss.
- Mrs Okafor 🖍️: marks every pile on her table with a red-pen blur ✍️.
- Gaz ⚽: the PE teacher, naps, and uses anything on his table as a pillow 😴.
- Caretaker 🧹: empties the recycling at 3:10.

**Day:**
- 2:45: Okafor goes to the kettle (2 min).
- 3:00: Okafor counts her pile 🤨📚.
- 3:10: recycling run.
- 3:15: the Head comes in for biscuits.
- 3:30: the bell. Piles go back to their owners.

**Routes:**
1. **Slip them onto Okafor's pile** during the kettle trip. Trap: put them there before 3:00 and her count sees the taller pile.
2. **Swap piles:** put your pile on Gaz's table and take his already-marked pile while he naps. Trap: he turns over in his sleep every ~4 min.
3. **Recycle, then claim the "already marked" crate** the caretaker sets down at 3:10.

**Lesson:** a thing you put down gets handled by whoever owns that spot. The fail: your essays on Gaz's table become a pillow, with a drool mark 💧. The Head sees it 😠👉.
**Engine:** held items, glances, owners · NEW `place item` (put a held item on a surface; that surface's owner acts on it) · level-only: Okafor's 3:00 count
**Funny because:** the hardest-working teacher in school is your printer.

## Final Call (airport gate)
**Card:** 💺 Gate closes 6:00. Get Cousin Mo into seat 1A.
**Cast:**
- Supervisor 🎧: headset, stands at the desk and is the boss.
- Mr Loud 😤: holds 1A and complains at the desk every 10 min.
- Mo 🧳: your cousin, happily waves at you all loop.
- Pilot 👨‍✈️: walks through at 5:40 and takes the manifest.
- The boarding queue: a crowd of passengers that thins group by group.

**Day:**
- 5:20: group 1 boards, and the queue is thick.
- 5:30: the supervisor goes down the jet bridge (3 min).
- 5:40: the pilot takes the manifest.
- 5:45: group 4. The queue is nearly empty.
- 6:00: doors close.

**Routes:**
1. **Swap the boarding passes** at the desk while the group-1 crowd stands between it and the supervisor. Trap: wait until group 3 and the crowd has thinned, so he sees straight through.
2. **Flip the gate sign** while the supervisor is on the jet bridge. Mr Loud wanders off to the wrong gate. Trap: he's back at 5:50 if he sees the flip.
3. **Change the manifest** before the pilot takes it at 5:40.

**Lesson:** people block sight lines, so a crowd is cover. The fail: you swap passes at 5:48 with three passengers left, and the supervisor's cone runs straight through the gap.
**Engine:** vision cones, tattles, scheduled walks · NEW `bodies block sight` (NPC bodies occlude vision cones) · level-only: Mo waves 👋 at you (a free tell to others)
**Funny because:** everyone has been the person hiding from their boss behind a queue of strangers.

## Coat on the Chair (call centre)
**Card:** ☎️ 5:00 PM. Lowest call count works the weekend. Don't be lowest.
**Cast:**
- Floor manager 📈: stands on a raised platform with a long view of the room and is the boss.
- Darren 🎧: the top seller, wears a headset, tattles.
- Priya ☕: on the next desk, takes a 5-minute break every 20 min.
- Wallboard 📊: a desk light per agent, 🟢 on a call or 🔴 idle.

**Day:**
- 4:00: everyone is on calls.
- 4:15: the manager walks the aisles (6 min).
- 4:30: Darren goes to the loo.
- 4:40: the manager has a 1:1 in the glass room.
- 5:00: the light tally. The reddest desk gets the weekend 😩.

**Routes:**
1. **Decoy.** Put your coat on your chair. From the platform it reads as you, typing. Then go and take Darren's headset off the hook so his light goes 🔴. Trap: the manager's 4:15 walk passes close and sees an empty coat.
2. **Hold music.** Put your phone on hold and leave it off the hook. Your light stays 🟢. Trap: Priya hears the hold music on her break and turns ❗👉.
3. **Swap the light cable** during the 1:1.

**Lesson:** something you leave behind can stand in for you, but only from far away. The fail: on the 4:15 walk the manager stops at your coat, lifts it, and 😠👉.
**Engine:** suspicion, tattles, owners · NEW `place item` (coat on chair) · NEW `decoy` (a placed item counts as you for viewers beyond N tiles) · level-only: the wallboard lights
**Funny because:** the coat on the chair is the oldest trick in the office.

## Yellow Sticker (supermarket)
**Card:** 🍩 Close at 10. Eat the last jam doughnut before Brenda does.
**Cast:**
- Manager 👔: stands at customer service and is the boss.
- Brenda 🏷️: puts yellow stickers on the bakery reductions at 9:45, then takes the doughnut herself.
- Guard Stan 📺: sits in the back office watching the CCTV monitors.
- CCTV dome 🔴: over the bakery, panning on a fixed rhythm. Its red light shows which way it faces.

**Day:**
- 9:00: Stan is at his monitors.
- 9:20: the manager sweeps the aisles.
- 9:30: Stan covers the tills (out of the office until 9:40).
- 9:45: Brenda's yellow-sticker round.
- 10:00: close.

**Routes:**
1. **Blind spot.** Eat while the dome pans away. Trap: jam on your sweater, and Brenda reads it 🍩❗👉.
2. **Empty monitors.** Eat during 9:30–9:40, when Stan is at the tills and nobody watches the screens.
3. **Turn the dome.** Stack a display tower of cereal boxes so the camera sees boxes. Trap: the 9:20 sweep sees the stacking.

**Lesson:** a camera is a witness who reports to someone else. The fail: you eat right under the dome and nothing happens… then at 9:30 Stan walks out, points at the monitor and at you 📺👉🍩.
**Engine:** sneaky actions, evidence marks, tattle-later · NEW `remote eye` (a camera with a scheduled cone; what it sees counts as seen by whoever is at its monitor) · NEW `place item` (box tower) · level-only: none
**Funny because:** staff vs. the yellow-sticker trolley is a real war.

## Lights Out (museum night shift)
**Card:** 🦖 3:00 AM. You put a party hat on the T-rex. Get it off before the curator's check.
**Cast:**
- Night chief 🔦: does a torch patrol (a moving cone) and is the boss.
- Curator 🧐: working late, does her round of the halls at 3:00.
- Cleaner Rosa 🧹: mops on a loop and hums. She stops and tattles.
- Breaker box ⚡: in the corridor.

**Day:**
- 2:00: the chief patrols the east halls.
- 2:20: Rosa mops the dinosaur hall.
- 2:35: the chief brews tea in the guard room (5 min).
- 2:45: the curator leaves her office.
- 3:00: the curator stands under the T-rex.

**Routes:**
1. **Kill the lights.** Pull the breaker and the halls go dark. Everyone's sight shrinks to their torch, or to 2 tiles. Climb the T-rex in the dark. Trap: the chief walks to the breaker ⚡❓ and the lights come back in 4 min.
2. **Sarcophagus.** Hide inside it while the torch passes, then climb during the tea break.
3. **Re-hat.** Move the hat onto the caveman mannequin. The curator stares at the caveman, not at you.

**Lesson:** darkness shrinks sight, except for the torch. The fail: in the dark you walk into the chief's torch beam 🔦❗.
**Engine:** vision cones, scene of the crime · NEW `lights` (a switch makes rooms dark; dark shrinks sight radius; NPCs go to fix it) · NEW `hide spot` · NEW `place item` · level-only: none
**Funny because:** the dignified T-rex in a party hat, and a night guard doing slapstick in the dark.

## Extra #4 (film set)
**Card:** 🎬 You're an extra. Get in the close-up before wrap.
**Cast:**
- AD 🎧: headset and clipboard, herds extras back to the holding pen. This is the "boss": being caught means the pen.
- Director 🎬: beret, sits at the monitor and sees only what the camera sees.
- Star 🕶️: in her trailer. Her hero hat sits on her chair.
- Camera op 🎥: rides a dolly on tracks with fixed moves. The camera's cone is the frame.

**Day:**
- 2:40: take 1 (red light, 🎬 clap).
- 3:00: the star goes back to her trailer.
- 3:10: take 2.
- 3:30: the star comes out for the close-up.
- 3:40: take 3, the close-up, then wrap.

**Routes:**
1. **The hat.** Wear the star's hat and stand in the frame for take 3. The director sees "her" in the close-up. Trap: the star wants the hat at 3:30 😠🎩.
2. **Ruin a take.** Knock over a C-stand during take 2 💥. The star storms off 😤 and the director takes the nearest extra in frame. Trap: the AD sees you by the stand.
3. **Ride the dolly.** Stand on the track at take 1's end mark.

**Lesson:** a camera cone you *want* to be in. The director sees only through it. The fail: you stand in front of the director himself. He stares through you at his monitor 📺, and the AD drags you to the pen.
**Engine:** vision cones, owners, scene of the crime · NEW `remote eye` (the camera feeds the director) · NEW `disguise` (the hat) · `noise` (the C-stand) · level-only: none
**Funny because:** every extra's dream is to be famous for one second, as a hat.

## Mechanics tally

| NEW generic mechanic | Sketches | Count |
|---|---|---|
| `place item` (put a held item on a surface; the surface's owner acts on it) | Staff Room, Coat on the Chair, Yellow Sticker, Lights Out (plus A1's mug-planting) | 4 |
| `hide spot` (a zone where you can't be seen, with a timer and a mark) | The Walk-In, Lights Out | 2 |
| `disguise` (a worn item = a role to non-owners, theft to the owner) | Handover, Extra #4 | 2 |
| `remote eye` (a camera cone whose sightings count for whoever watches its monitor) | Yellow Sticker, Extra #4 | 2 |
| `noise(x,y,r)` (already seeded by Weekend Shift) | Extra #4 | 1 |
| `bodies block sight` (NPC bodies occlude vision cones) | Final Call | 1 |
| `door occludes` (an open swing door blocks sight) | The Walk-In | 1 |
| `decoy` (a placed item counts as you to distant viewers; builds on `place item`) | Coat on the Chair | 1 |
| `lights` (a switch makes rooms dark, sight shrinks, NPCs go to fix it) | Lights Out | 1 |

Build order suggestion: `place item` first (4 + A1), then `hide spot` / `disguise` / `remote eye`.
`bodies block sight` and `door occludes` are one occlusion change and could ship together.
