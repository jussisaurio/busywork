# The Last Bus

## Card
3:00 PM. The last bus leaves at 4:30. The boss leaves at 4:50.
Nobody leaves before him.

## Name
"The Last Bus": your bus leaves at 4:30, twenty minutes before the boss does, and nobody leaves before him. The name and
the card say what you want and why it is a problem. They say nothing about how you get out of it.

## The situation
You work in an open-plan office. The last bus home stops outside the lobby's glass door at 4:30. The boss goes home at
4:50 sharp, every day, and the office rule is that nobody leaves before him. His coat hangs on a rack in the hallway,
by his office door: while it hangs there, he is in. If nothing changes, at 4:30 the boss is still at his desk, so you
stay at yours and watch your bus drive away. If he has gone home by 4:30, nothing keeps you, and you get on the bus if
you are at the stop. The day is a time loop: when you lose, it is 3:00 again.

## The rules
- **Everyone lives by the clock of the room he is in.** There are two wall clocks: the boss's clock in his office
  (behind his chair) and the open-plan clock on the hallway wall (over Kev's desk, on the boss's way to the kitchen).
  A person looks up at the clock of the room he stands in, thinks of its time (a clock face) and from then on believes
  it. If the clock has moved since his last look, he thinks the face and 😮: he believes it. Nobody acts against a
  clock he has just read, and whoever reads a clock stands near it. Every time below is what the person believes.
- **Nobody leaves before the boss.** He takes his coat only when he goes home. At 3:13 Kev picks up his laptop to go,
  looks at the coat on the rack (🧥😬) and puts the laptop down again. At their 4:50, Lin and Kev stand at their desks
  and look at the rack; while the coat hangs there they think 🧥😬 and sit down again. When it has gone, they wave
  and follow him out.
- **He checks your desk.** After each look at his clock the boss looks out through the glass at your desk. You there:
  your face, ✔️. Your chair empty: your face, ❓, and he comes looking for you: his office, the open plan, the lobby,
  the bus stop. If he finds you inside on the phone, he lets it go. If he sees you out in the street while he is still
  at work, you are caught.
- **He never ignores his own clock.** If his clock jumps while you are in his office, he looks from the clock to you
  (your face, 🤨) and climbs up to set it right (the time, angry).

## Cast
- **The caretaker** (blue work jacket). At 3:00 he is up the stepladder under the boss's clock, changing its battery
  (🔋). By 3:09 he has gone, and he leaves both stepladders where they stand.
- **The boss** (navy suit, shirtsleeves until he goes home; glass office top left). He sits with his back to his clock,
  facing the glass and the open plan.
  - 3:00 to 3:15: out at a long lunch. He comes back along the street, past the bus stop and in through the lobby, and
    sits down at about 3:22. The first thing he does is turn round to his clock, then he looks at your desk.
  - He turns round to his clock again at 3:35, back from his break at about 4:00, at 4:10 and at 4:25, each time
    followed by a look at your desk.
  - 3:40 to 3:55: his break. On the way to the kitchen he walks under the open-plan clock (about 3:43) and reads it:
    if it does not say home time, he thinks the face and ⏳ and walks on. He pours a coffee (3:47). Walking back he
    reads the open-plan clock again (about 3:58).
  - 4:50: he takes his coat off the rack, waves, and goes home through the lobby and over the zebra crossing.
  - Told about a stepladder, he sends you to your desk with 20 minutes of work and climbs up to set the pushed clocks
    right.
- **Lin** (teal cardigan, the desk below the boss's glass wall, five steps from the open-plan clock). She sits facing
  the glass and sees the boss's stepladder through it. Anyone who climbs it gets told on.
  - She looks up at the open-plan clock at 3:12, 3:50, 4:10 and 4:25, and each time she sits down.
  - 3:20: she goes to the restroom (🚻, bottom right). She is out of sight until about 3:35.
  - 4:50: she checks the boss's coat (see the rules).
- **Kev** (olive hoodie, the desk right under the open-plan clock, facing it). Anyone on that stepladder is in front of
  his eyes.
  - 3:01 to about 3:10: a coffee from the pot in the kitchen (top right). He goes again at 3:25, back at about 3:34.
  - He looks up at the open-plan clock at 3:01, 3:10, 3:20, 3:55, 4:10 and 4:25, and each time he sits down.
  - 3:13: the laptop and the coat (see the rules).
  - 4:50: he checks the boss's coat, the same as Lin.
- **You** (red jumper, the middle desk). You can climb the stepladder under either clock and push the clock 10 minutes
  forward. Each push takes a moment, moves the hands on 10 minutes, paints the extra time red on the face and makes
  the clock hang a little more crooked. Walking about you take a phone call ('pretend'); at your desk you type.
  - If the boss turns round to his clock while you are on its stepladder, you are caught.
  - If Lin or Kev sees you on a stepladder, they shout and point at you (!, finger, you). If the boss is in, he sends
    you back to your desk with 20 minutes of work and the push you were doing does not count. If he is out, they tell
    him when he walks back in (finger, clock), and he sets his clock right.

## Who does what
The caretaker is there before you can do anything. At 3:00 he stands on the stepladder under the boss's clock and
puts a new battery in it. He did the open-plan clock first. He leaves both stepladders where they stand and goes out
through the lobby by 3:09. He does not care what anyone else does.

The boss is out at a long lunch when the day starts. At about 3:15 he walks back along the street, past the bus stop
and in through the lobby. He sits down in his glass office at about 3:22 with his back to his clock. He turns round to
it, then looks out at your desk. He does that again at 3:35, at about 4:00, at 4:10 and at 4:25. If your chair is
empty, he gets up and looks for you. At 3:40 he goes for a coffee. He reads the open-plan clock on his way to the
kitchen, pours a coffee at the pot at 3:47 and reads the clock again on his way back. At 4:50 by the clock in front of
him, he takes his coat off the rack in the hallway, waves and goes home over the zebra crossing.

Lin works at the desk below the boss's glass wall. She faces it, so she sees his stepladder through the glass. She
looks up at the open-plan clock every quarter of an hour or so. At 3:20 she goes to the restroom and comes back at
about 3:35. If she sees someone on a stepladder, she shouts and points. She tells the boss at once if he is in, or
when he walks back in. At her 4:50 she stands up and looks at the coat rack. She sits down again while the coat is
there, and she follows the boss out when it has gone.

Kev works at the desk right under the open-plan clock, facing it. At 3:01 he goes to the kitchen for a coffee and comes
back at about 3:10. At 3:13 he picks up his laptop to go home, sees the boss's coat on the rack, puts the laptop down
and sits down again. At 3:25 he goes for another coffee and comes back at about 3:34. Like Lin, he tells on anyone
he sees on a stepladder. At his 4:50 he looks at the coat rack and does what Lin does.

## Things in the office
- **The boss's wall clock**, in his office behind his chair. He keeps the time by it. It is the clock of route 1.
- **The open-plan clock**, on the hallway wall over Kev's desk and on the boss's way to the kitchen. Lin and Kev keep
  the time by it, and the boss reads it when he walks under it. It is the clock of route 2.
- **Two stepladders**, one under each clock. The caretaker leaves them there after the battery change. They are how
  you reach a clock, and anyone on one is easy to see.
- **The coat rack and the boss's coat**, in the hallway by his office door. The coat on the rack means he is in. It
  shows the rule (Kev at 3:13, Lin and Kev at their 4:50) and shows when he has gone.
- **The glass wall** of the boss's office. He sees your desk through it, and Lin sees his stepladder through it.
- **The desks**: the boss's desk, yours, Lin's and Kev's. Your empty chair is what makes him look for you. The empty
  desks at the sides make the office feel real.
- **The kitchen** with the coffee pot, the fridge and a table. Kev goes there at 3:01, which is your time on the
  open-plan clock. The boss goes there on his break, which takes him under that clock.
- **The restroom.** Lin goes there at 3:20, which is your time on the boss's clock.
- **The plants.** They make the office feel real and do nothing else.
- **The lobby, the glass door, the bus stop sign, the road and the zebra crossing.** The bus stops at the sign at 4:30.
  The boss comes back from lunch past it and goes home over the crossing, so he sees anyone waiting at the stop
  before he has gone.
- **The bus.** It pulls in at about 4:26 and leaves at 4:30.

## Ways to win
You win when the boss has gone home before 4:30 and you are at the bus stop at 4:30. The bus pulls in at 4:26 and you
get on. You see the stop at the start (a bus stop sign by the road, outside the lobby's glass door) and the bus at the
kerb (shot `bus-arrives`).

### 1. Push the boss's clock (route icon: clock)
Steps:
1. After his first look (about 3:22), while Lin is in the restroom, leave your desk on the phone and walk into the
   boss's office.
2. Climb the stepladder behind his chair and push his clock three times (+30).
3. Get out of his office and back to your desk before he turns round at 3:35.
4. At 3:35 he turns round, sees 4:05 and believes it (😮), then sees you at your desk (✔️). From now on he lives half
   an hour early: he skips his coffee, takes his coat at about 4:21 and walks out through the lobby; he is over the
   crossing by about 4:28.
5. Follow him out, behind him, and wait at the stop.

In a real office: if someone sets the office clock half an hour fast, a boss who goes home by his wall clock goes home
half an hour early. A boss who sees you next to his clock as it jumps knows what you did.

The facts, and when you see them:
- **The clocks are set by somebody else.** At 3:00 the caretaker is up the boss's stepladder with a battery (shot
  `start`); he leaves the stepladders behind.
- **The boss goes by his wall clock.** At 3:22 he turns round to it with a clock thought, then checks your desk (shots
  `boss-back-from-lunch`, `boss-glances-at-your-desk`).
- **You can push a clock forward.** Up close each is a real wall clock with a stepladder under it (shots
  `boss-clock-close`, `open-clock-close`). After you push it, the hands read later than the HUD clock, the extra time
  is red and it hangs crooked (shots `open-clock-pushed`, `boss-clock-pushed`).
- **His back is to his clock.** His chair faces away from the stepladder and his vision cone points away from it
  (shots `boss-back-from-lunch`, `boss-clock-pushed`).
- **Lin watches his stepladder.** Her vision cone reaches through the glass to it (shot `start`). Try while she is at
  her desk and she points and shouts (shot `lin-tells`); she tells the boss when he walks in, and he sets the clock
  right (shot `lin-tells-the-boss`).
- **Lin is away from 3:20.** You see her get up and walk off to the restroom (shot `lin-goes-to-the-restroom`).
- **He turns round at 3:35.** Too late on the stepladder, and he catches you there (shot `end-caught`). Still in his
  office, and he looks at you and sets it right (shots `boss-suspects`, `boss-sets-it-right`). In time, he believes his
  clock (shot `boss-jumps`).
- **His coat means he is in.** At 3:13 Kev looks at it and stays (shot `kev-sees-the-coat`); the boss puts it on when
  he goes (shot `boss-takes-his-coat`).
- **+30 is enough and +20 is not.** The card gives the sums: he leaves at 4:50 by his clock and your bus goes at 4:30,
  and he needs a few minutes to walk out. With +20 he is still at his desk at 4:30.
- **Don't go before him.** Leave your desk while he is in and he finds your chair empty and comes looking (shot
  `boss-hunts`); out in the street, he catches you (shot `end-caught-in-the-street`).

### 2. Push the open-plan clock (route icon: coffee)
Steps:
1. At 3:01, when Kev gets up for his coffee, climb the stepladder over his desk.
2. Push the open-plan clock six times (+60). You are done by about 3:08, before Kev comes back.
3. Go back to your desk.

Kev sits down at 3:10, looks up and believes it (😮): he thinks it is 4:10. Lin looks at 3:12 and believes it too (she
skips the restroom today). At about 3:43 the boss walks under the open-plan clock on his way to the kitchen: it says
4:43, and he walks on (⏳). At 3:50, when the open-plan clock says 4:50, Lin and Kev stand and look at the coat rack
(🧥😬): the coat still hangs there, because he is in the kitchen on his break, so they stay. At about 3:58 the boss
walks back under the open-plan clock, reads 4:58 and believes it (😮). He takes his coat at about 4:00 and waves; Lin
and Kev see the rack empty, wave and follow him out. All three are gone by about 4:09. Go out to the stop after them.

In a real office: if the hallway clock says it is home time, people look up and believe it, and so does a boss who walks
past it with a coffee in his hand. Nobody checks a wall clock against his phone.

The facts, and when you see them:
- **Lin and Kev go by the open-plan clock.** At 3:01 Kev looks up at it with a clock thought before he gets up (shot
  `kev-checks-the-clock`). At 3:12 Lin looks up at it too (shot `lin-checks-the-open-clock`).
- **Kev is away at 3:01.** You see him walk off to the kitchen and pour a coffee (shots `open-clock-close`,
  `kev-pours-a-coffee`). His 😮 when he comes back shows that the push took (shot `kev-jumps`).
- **They will not go before him.** At 3:13 Kev looks at the coat and stays (shot `kev-sees-the-coat`); at their 4:50
  they look at it and stay (shot `lin-checks-the-coat`).
- **The boss reads the open-plan clock when he walks under it.** On his way to his coffee (shot
  `boss-reads-the-open-clock-going-out`), at the pot (shot `boss-pours-a-coffee`), and on his way back.
- **At 4:50 or later he goes, and they follow.** He believes the clock (shot `boss-reads-the-open-clock`), takes his
  coat (shot `boss-takes-his-coat-early`), and Lin and Kev follow him out (shot `lin-and-kev-follow`).
- **+60 is enough and +50 is not.** At 3:58 +50 shows 4:48, and he walks on.

## Ways to lose
- **The boss is still in at 4:30, and you are at your desk.** The end card: 4:30 on the boss's clock; he is at his desk
  in his shirtsleeves with his coat on the rack, so you stay; out of the window your bus drives off (shot `end-fail`).
- **The boss is still in at 4:30, and you are at the stop.** You cannot get on while he is in, and he fetches you back
  to your desk. The end card: you at your desk, the boss pointing at you, his coat glowing on its rack, and out of the
  window the bus pulls away (shot `end-fail-at-the-stop`).
- **Caught on the stepladder.** He turns round to his clock while you are up it. The end card: you up the stepladder
  at his clock, and the boss below pointing up at you, angry (shot `end-caught`).
- **Caught in the street.** Out at the stop while he is still at work: he passes it on his way back from lunch, or
  comes looking for you after seeing your empty chair. The end card, in daylight with no bus: you at the stop, the
  boss pointing at you from the lobby door, his coat glowing on its rack through the window (shot
  `end-caught-in-the-street`).
- **The boss went home early, but you were not at the stop at 4:30.** The end card: you at your desk in the empty
  office, the coat rack empty, and out of the window the bus pulls away (shot `end-blamed`).
- **Told on.** Not a loss by itself. Lin or Kev points at you, the boss sends you to your desk with 20 minutes of work
  and sets his clock right, and usually the window has closed when you are free.

## The joke
3:50 to 4:09 on the open-plan route. Two grown adults stand up at their desks, stare at a coat on a rack, grimace and
sit down again. Eight minutes later the boss, whose rule keeps them there, strolls back from his coffee, glances at the
hallway clock, believes it, and fetches his coat in a hurry. He is not out of the office before they are up and following
him to the door.
