# The Audit

## Card
3:00 PM. A laptop audit at 3:30. There should be four.
You sold yours. Make him count four.

## Name
"The Audit". A man with a clipboard comes to count the office's laptops, and the level is that count, from the
first tick to his signature. The name says what happens at 3:30 before you have solved anything.

## The situation
You work in a small open-plan office: the boss's glass office, three desks in the hallway row (Lin's, yours, Kev's),
a kitchen, a lobby with the lift. Every desk is supposed to have a company laptop. You sold yours.

At 3:30 an auditor counts them. He walks the desks in a fixed order (the boss's, Lin's, yours, Kev's) and judges
each desk when he gets to it: a laptop on it is a tick, no laptop is a cross. A counted desk keeps a green sticker
with a tick on its front edge. At your empty desk he gets angry, points you to the lift, and you leave with your
things in a box.

He only counts what is on a desk at the moment he looks. A laptop he has already ticked off can be moved to a desk
he has not reached yet, and he counts it again. If he counts four, he comes back to your desk at 3:46, smiles and
signs the audit off. Nobody learns that your laptop is gone.

## Cast
- **You.** You sit at the middle desk of the hallway row. There is no laptop on your desk, only a monitor and a
  keyboard.
- **The auditor** (grey suit, clipboard). From outside the company.
  - 3:00-3:25: on his way up; you do not see him.
  - 3:25: out of the lift, a few minutes early. He gets a coffee at the hallway coffee machine (3:28-3:30).
  - 3:30: the audit. He stands behind each desk, faces it, holds up his clipboard, and a sign goes up over the desk:
    the boss's at 3:32.8, Lin's at 3:36.0 (he takes longer at hers), yours at 3:40.2, Kev's at 3:43.5. With each
    tick a green sticker goes on the desk's front edge and stays there. He looks only where his list tells him to
    look, and walks on facing forward.
  - 3:44: back to your desk to sign off. He arrives at 3:46.4 and smiles if he counted four.
- **The boss** (navy suit). Sits in his glass office, his back to the glass, facing his window.
  - 3:08: goes down to the lobby for a smoke (there by about 3:12). His laptop stays on his desk.
  - 3:16: back (at his desk at about 3:20). If his laptop is gone, he wonders where it went.
  - 3:33: his desk ticked off, he goes for another smoke and stays in the lobby until 3:44, when everyone goes back
    to their desks for the sign-off. A smoker who has done his part.
- **Lin** (teal cardigan). Her desk faces the glass wall of the boss's office, so she looks straight into it.
  - 3:13: the restroom, far east of the office. Back at her desk at 3:24. If her laptop is gone, she wonders where it
    went.
  - 3:35: coffee at the hallway machine (3:37-3:38), then back to her desk at 3:39.7. Same check.
- **Kev** (olive hoodie). Your friendly neighbour at the east desk.
  - 3:09.5: waves and goes to lunch in the lift. His laptop stays on his desk.
  - 3:21.9: back from lunch, he stops at the end of your desk for a word (a wave). If his laptop is lying on your
    desk, he picks it up instead, surprised, and carries it home to his desk (3:23.5).
  - 3:24: to the kitchen for a coffee. He stands at the kitchen counter with it until 3:37.4. His laptop stays on his
    desk.
  - 3:40.7: back, by your desk again on the way to his own (the same wave, or the same pick-up).

Owners mind their laptops. Anyone who sees you unplug a laptop stops you and points you out. An owner who sees you
carry theirs takes it back, angry.

## Ways to win
Each route ends the same way: a borrowed laptop is on your desk when he ticks it at 3:40.2, he counts four, and you
are at your desk when he comes to sign off at 3:46.4.

Facts that all three routes need:
- **Laptops can be unplugged, carried and put on your desk.** Seen at any time: standing next to any laptop shows the
  hand prompt; holding one beside your desk shows the put-down prompt.
- **He ticks the desks one by one, in order, and judges each one when he gets there.** Seen in the first loop,
  3:30-3:40: the boss's desk gets a laptop-and-tick sign at 3:32.8, Lin's at 3:36.0, and your empty desk gets the
  laptop-and-cross sign at 3:40.2. Each sign goes up the moment he arrives at that desk, and each ticked desk keeps
  a green sticker after the sign is gone.
- **He does not look back at a desk he has ticked.** Seen at 3:33 and 3:36: after each tick he turns his back on that
  desk and walks down the hallway, his view cone pointing ahead of him. The green sticker stays on the desk: it is
  counted, whatever is on it now.
- **He must not see you with a laptop.** Seen any time he is on the floor: his view cone. In the first loop, his cone
  turns red on you at 3:40.2.

### The boss's laptop (counted on his desk, then on yours)
Steps: let him tick the boss's desk (3:32.8). Wait for the boss to leave (3:33) and for Lin to go for coffee (3:35).
Walk into the boss's office, unplug his laptop (3:35.1), put it on your desk, sit. He ticks your desk at 3:40.2.
- **The boss leaves his ticked laptop alone.** Seen at 3:33-3:34: right after his tick, the boss walks out of his
  office and down to the lobby. His laptop is still on his desk, next to its sticker, and he is still smoking in the
  lobby when your desk is ticked at 3:40.2.
- **Lin watches the boss's office, and she leaves at 3:35.** Seen from 3:00: Lin sits facing the glass, and her view
  cone reaches through it into the office. At 3:08-3:16 the boss's office is empty, but she is at her desk looking
  in. At 3:35 she gets up and walks to the hallway coffee machine.

### Lin's laptop (counted on her desk, then on yours)
Steps: stand in the hallway right beside where he ticks Lin's desk. He ticks it at 3:36.0 while she is getting
coffee. Unplug her laptop behind his back (3:36.1), carry it east to your desk ahead of him, put it down, sit.
- **Her desk is ticked while she is away.** Seen in the first loop at 3:35-3:36: Lin walks to the coffee machine,
  and the tick sign goes up over her desk while her chair is empty.
- **Beside him is outside his view.** Seen at 3:36: while he ticks her desk his view cone points down at the desk,
  not sideways along the hallway.
- **Lin does not find her laptop on your desk.** Seen at 3:24 if you take it while she is in the restroom: she comes
  back, sits at her empty desk and wonders (laptop, question mark), but she stays at her desk at the far west end,
  out of sight of yours. (That early version loses: her desk gets the cross at 3:36.)

### Kev's laptop (counted on your desk, then on his)
Steps: after Kev goes to the kitchen (3:24), unplug his laptop from his desk, put it on yours, sit. He ticks your
desk at 3:40.2. At 3:40.7 Kev comes back, takes his laptop from your desk and plugs it in at his own desk before the
auditor gets there (3:43.5): the fourth tick.
- **Kev stops at your desk on his way back.** Seen at 3:21.9: back from lunch, he stops at the end of your desk and
  waves before he goes on to his own desk.
- **If his laptop is on your desk, he takes it home.** Seen if you try it at lunch (put his laptop on your desk
  between 3:10 and 3:21): at 3:21.9 he picks it up (laptop, exclamation mark) and carries it back to his desk.
- **He is back from the kitchen just after your desk is checked.** Seen from 3:24 to 3:37: Kev stands in the
  kitchen with a coffee. Seen in the first loop at 3:40.2: in the moment your desk gets its cross, Kev is walking
  back down the hallway toward your desk.
- **His desk comes after yours.** Seen in the first loop: the order is the boss's, Lin's, yours, and Kev's is the
  only desk left.

## Ways to lose
- **A desk has no laptop when he gets to it.** The laptop-and-cross sign goes up over that desk, he bursts out angry
  and his view cone turns red on you. End picture: that desk (yours, or Lin's or Kev's if you moved theirs too early
  and it did not come back) with the cross sign over it; the auditor points; you walk to the lift with a box.
- **He sees you with a laptop** (unplugging it, or carrying it). He bursts out (laptop, exclamation mark). End
  picture: the laptop on the floor, the auditor points, you walk to the lift with a box.
- **You are away from your desk when he comes to sign off at 3:46.** The count passed, but your chair is empty. End
  picture: a light on your empty chair, the auditor glaring at it and asking where you are (your face, question
  mark), you walk to the lift with a box.
- Being stopped by an owner is not a loss by itself: the laptop goes back on its desk, and the loop usually ends with
  the cross at your desk.
- The code has a fourth end ("fail": the audit never finishes, a clock and a puzzled auditor). No play reaches it.

## The joke
Kev's route. Kev comes back from the kitchen, finds his laptop on your desk, and carries it home, humming, a few steps
behind the auditor's back. Your desk has just been ticked with it; a minute later Kev's desk is ticked with it too.
The win picture: you smug behind an empty desk, the auditor beaming over four ticks, and Kev at his desk with the
laptop that was counted twice, thanks to him.
