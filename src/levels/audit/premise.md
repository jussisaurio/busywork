# The Audit

## Card
3:00 PM. Laptop audit at 3:30.
You sold yours.

## Name
"The Audit". A man with a clipboard comes to count the office's laptops, and the level is that count, from the
first tick to his signature. The name says what happens at 3:30 before you have solved anything.

## The situation
You work in a small open-plan office: the boss's glass office, three desks in the hallway row (Lin's, yours, Kev's),
a kitchen, a lobby with the lift. Every desk is supposed to have a company laptop. You sold yours.

At 3:30 an auditor counts them. He walks the desks in a fixed order (the boss's, Lin's, yours, Kev's) and judges
each desk when he gets to it: a laptop on it is a tick, no laptop is a cross. A counted desk keeps a sticker on its
front edge, green with a tick or red with a cross. A cross makes him angry, but he finishes his list; then he comes
back to your desk to sign off, and with a cross on his list he points you to the lift, and you leave with your
things in a box.

He only counts what is on a desk at the moment he looks. A laptop he has already ticked off can be moved to a desk
he has not reached yet, and he counts it again. If he counts four, he comes back to your desk at 3:59.9, smiles and
signs the audit off. Nobody learns that your laptop is gone.

## Cast
- **You** (red jumper). You sit at the middle desk of the hallway row. There is no laptop on your desk, only a
  monitor, a keyboard and a loose cable. Walking about, you can take a phone call ('pretend'); at your desk you type.
  You cannot pretend with a laptop in your hands: a laptop is too big to hide, sitting or walking.
- **The auditor** (grey suit and tie, short grey hair, glasses, moustache, a badge on a lanyard, clipboard). From
  outside the company. Every laptop is company property to him: he must never see one in your hands.
- **The boss** (navy suit). Sits in his glass office, his back to the glass, facing his window. A smoker.
- **Lin** (teal cardigan). Her desk faces the glass wall of the boss's office, so she looks straight into it.
- **Kev** (olive hoodie). Your neighbour at the east desk, who leaves his things about.

## Who does what
The auditor is on his way up until 3:25. He comes out of the lift a few minutes early and has a coffee at the
hallway coffee machine. At 3:30 he starts his list. He stands behind each desk, faces it, and a sign goes up over
the desk: the boss's at 3:32.8, Lin's at 3:36.3. At Lin's desk his pen runs dry, every loop (a thought: pen,
question mark). He shakes it and scribbles on the corner of his clipboard, then gives up and walks off to the meeting
room (3:38.5). He takes a pen from the mug on the meeting table, his back to the door (3:42-3:43.7), and walks to
your desk: its sign goes up at 3:44.9. The new pen is dry too (a thought: pen, fuming, 3:45.2): he shakes it,
scribbles, shakes it again, and at last it writes (3:47.3). Then his phone rings (3:47.8, a phone at his ear). He
takes the call at the meeting-room window, his back to the door and the office, talking (3:50.4-3:52.3). Then he
walks to Kev's desk (3:56.6), the last on his list. At 3:57.8 he heads back to your desk to sign off, and he is there at 3:59.9: a smile
for four ticks; for a cross, a thought of the cross (laptop, cross), then angry, pointing at you. He looks only where
his list tells him to look, and he walks facing forward. A cross makes him angry, but he finishes his list.
While he fights a dry pen (at Lin's desk 3:36.6-3:38, at yours 3:45.2-3:47.3) he is head down over his clipboard: his
view cone shrinks to the clipboard, and he sees nothing past it, not even a hand at his elbow.

The boss goes down to the lobby for a smoke at 3:06. He smokes facing the lift and seldom looks round. His laptop
stays on his desk. He is back at his desk at 3:17.8. If his laptop is gone, he wonders where it went (laptop,
question mark) and walks the hallway looking for it until 3:22.5. If he sees it on your desk, he glares at it (angry,
eyes, laptop), walks to your desk, picks it up there (angry, laptop) and carries it home. He is behind his office
door with it before the auditor is on the floor. At 3:33, his desk ticked off, he goes for another smoke and stays in
the lobby through the sign-off.

Lin goes to the restroom at 3:11 and is back at her desk at 3:22. If her laptop is gone, she wonders where it went
(laptop, question mark) and stays at her desk: the auditor is due, and he is out of the lift three minutes later. At
3:35 she goes for a coffee in the kitchen and stays at the pot through the sign-off.

Kev goes to lunch at 3:10, every loop. He packs his laptop, stops at the end of your desk, puts it down to wave
goodbye (a wave), and goes to the lift without it. Back at 3:19.9, he finds it on your desk and slaps his forehead
(laptop, facepalm): silly him. He carries it home to his desk. If it is not there (you took it), he wonders where he
left it. At 3:24 he has a coffee in the kitchen, in view; his laptop stays on his desk. At 3:42.4 he finishes it and
walks back to his desk. If his laptop is not on it, he wonders where it went (laptop, question mark, 3:44) and looks
over at your desk. If it is there, he strolls over, not hiding anything: he is fetching his own laptop. The auditor
stands right there, head down over his dry pen. Kev picks the laptop up beside him, slaps his forehead again (laptop,
facepalm: mine, silly me, 3:46) and carries it home humming (a note), behind the auditor's back (home 3:48).

Anyone who comes home with his laptop and finds another laptop on his desk (you put it there) wonders at it
(laptop, question mark) and puts his own in a drawer, out of sight. Nobody holds a laptop at his desk.

Anyone who sees you unplug a laptop points at you, shocked, and you let go. Anyone but its owner tells the auditor
once he is on the floor (pointing at you, laptop), and he sends you to your desk for twenty minutes of paperwork:
you sit typing through the audit. An owner who sees you with his laptop takes it back, angry.

## Things in the office
- **The four desks** (the boss's, Lin's, yours, Kev's). Each takes any laptop. The auditor judges what is on each one
  when he gets to it; a counted desk keeps a sticker on its front edge, a green tick or a red cross.
- **Three laptops**, each lid in its owner's colour: navy (the boss), teal (Lin), olive (Kev). They can be unplugged,
  carried and put down on a desk. One in your hands is plain to see, even when you sit.
- **The glass wall** of the boss's office. Lin sees into his office through it from her desk.
- **The meeting room** with its long table, a mug of pens on it and a window. The auditor fetches a pen there and
  takes his phone call there, both times with his back to the door. Its walls are solid: from inside it, he does not
  see the hallway.
- **The lobby** with the ashtray and the lift. The boss smokes there. Its glass wall looks onto the open office.
- **The hallway coffee machine** and **the kitchen coffee pot.** Each pours a cup. With a cup in your hand, your
  hands are full until you drink it. The auditor has his coffee at the hallway machine; Kev and Lin have theirs in
  the kitchen.
- **The restroom.** Lin goes there at 3:11.
- **Lin's paper pile**, the plants, the coat stand. They make the office feel real and do nothing else.

## Ways to win
Each route ends the same way: a borrowed laptop is on your desk when he ticks it at 3:44.9, he counts four, and you
are at your desk when he comes to sign off at 3:59.9.

Facts that all three routes need:
- **Laptops can be unplugged, carried and put on your desk.** Seen at 3:11.7 every loop: Kev puts his down on your
  desk, and it lights up as something you can take. Holding one beside your desk lights up the place to put it.
- **He ticks the desks one by one, in order, and judges each one when he gets there.** Seen in the first loop,
  3:30-3:45: the boss's desk gets a laptop-and-tick sign at 3:32.8, Lin's at 3:36.3, and your empty desk gets the
  laptop-and-cross sign at 3:44.9. Each sign goes up the moment he arrives at that desk, and each desk keeps its
  sticker after the sign is gone.
- **He does not go back to a desk he has ticked.** Seen at 3:34 and 3:38: after each tick he turns his back on that
  desk and walks on. The sticker stays on the desk: it is counted, whatever is on it now.
- **He must not see you with a laptop.** Seen any time he is on the floor: his view cone, and the alert when you
  sit with one in your hands as he comes.

### The boss's laptop (counted on his desk, then on yours)
Steps: let him tick the boss's desk (3:32.8). The boss goes for his smoke (3:33) and Lin for her coffee (3:35). When
the auditor walks off to the meeting room for a pen (3:38.5), go into the boss's office and unplug his laptop
(3:39.6). Wait in the office until he is inside the meeting room (3:41.3), then put the laptop on your desk and sit.
He ticks your desk at 3:44.9.

In a real office: a man who has just ticked a laptop on a desk does not come back to check it, and an empty office
with its owner downstairs smoking is nobody's to watch. You move the laptop while the auditor is in another room,
looking for a pen.

- **The boss leaves his ticked laptop alone.** Seen at 3:33-3:34: right after his tick, the boss walks out of his
  office and down to the lobby. His laptop is still on his desk, next to its sticker.
- **Lin watches the boss's office, and she leaves at 3:35.** Seen from 3:00: Lin sits facing the glass, and her view
  cone reaches through it into the office. At 3:06-3:11 the boss's office is empty, but she is at her desk looking
  in. At 3:35 she gets up and walks to the kitchen.
- **His pen runs dry at Lin's desk, and he goes for another.** Seen every loop at 3:36.6-3:42: the pen thought, the
  scribbling, then the walk into the meeting room and the hand in the pen mug, his back to the door.

### Lin's laptop (counted on her desk, then on yours)
Steps: wait in the hallway. He ticks Lin's desk at 3:36.3 while she is getting coffee, and his pen runs dry. When he
gives up and walks off to the meeting room (3:38.5), unplug her laptop (3:39.6). Wait at her desk until he is inside
the meeting room (3:41.3), carry it to your desk, put it down, sit.

The cheeky version: take it at his elbow while he is head down shaking his dry pen (3:36.6-3:38), and get away down
the hallway before he looks up. Still standing at her desk when he does, and he sees you with it.

In a real office: an auditor who has to go and find a pen leaves the desk he has just counted, and he does not count
it again. Lin is at the coffee pot in the kitchen until after the sign-off: she does not see her desk.

- **Her desk is ticked while she is away.** Seen in the first loop at 3:35-3:36: Lin walks off to the kitchen, and
  the tick sign goes up over her desk while her chair is empty.
- **His pen runs dry at her desk, and he goes for another.** Seen every loop at 3:36.6-3:42, as above.
- **Head down over a dry pen, he is blind past his clipboard.** Seen every loop at 3:36.6-3:38: his view cone shrinks
  to the clipboard while he shakes and scribbles, and grows back when he looks up.
- **Lin stays in the kitchen.** Seen in the first loop: she is at the pot from 3:40 until the end.

### Kev's laptop (counted on your desk, then on his)
Steps: after Kev goes to the kitchen (3:24), unplug his laptop from his desk, put it on yours, sit. The auditor ticks
your desk at 3:44.9 and stands there fighting his second dry pen. Kev comes back, misses his laptop, sees it on your
desk, strolls over and takes it home (mine, silly me) right beside the head-down auditor, humming. The auditor ticks
it on Kev's desk at 3:56.6: the fourth tick.

In a real office: Kev leaves his laptop on your desk every day, so finding it there again is no surprise to him; he
picks it up openly and blames himself. The auditor, glaring at a pen that will not write, does not look up at a
colleague fetching his own laptop from a desk already ticked.

- **Kev leaves his laptop on your desk and comes back for it.** Seen every loop at 3:11.7 and 3:19.9.
- **Kev has his coffee in the kitchen, then goes back to his desk.** Seen in the first loop from 3:24 to 3:44.
- **The pen from the pot is dry too, and he stands head down over it at your desk.** Seen every loop at
  3:45.2-3:47.3: the fuming pen thought, the shaking and scribbling, his view cone shrunk to the clipboard.
- **His desk comes after yours.** Seen in the first loop: the order is the boss's, Lin's, yours, and Kev's is the
  only desk left.

## Ways to lose
- **A desk has no laptop when he gets to it.** The laptop-and-cross sign goes up over that desk and he is angry. He
  finishes his list, comes to your desk at 3:59.9 and points at you. End picture: that desk (yours, or Lin's or the
  boss's, with its own chair and mug, if you took theirs too early and kept it) with the cross sign over it; the auditor points; you walk to
  the lift with a box. If you take the boss's laptop too early, he takes it back from your desk himself (3:22), and
  the cross is at your desk.
- **He sees you with a laptop** (unplugging it, carrying it, or sitting with it in your hands). He bursts out (alert,
  pointing at you). Standing at a desk he is counting, he sees anyone who reaches for its laptop, from either side
  of the desk, unless he is head down over his dry pen; and when he looks up he sees you if you are still there. End picture: the laptop on
  the floor, the auditor points, you walk to the lift with a box.
- **You are away from your desk when he comes to sign off at 3:59.9.** The count passed, but your chair is empty. End
  picture: a light on your empty chair, the auditor glaring at it and asking where you are (your face, question
  mark), you walk to the lift with a box.
- Being stopped by a witness, told on, or having an owner take his laptop back is not a loss by itself, but each
  costs you something you see: the laptop goes home, or the auditor puts you on paperwork, and the loop usually ends
  with the cross at your desk.
- The code has a fourth end ("fail" with no desk: a clock and a puzzled auditor). No play reaches it.

## The joke
Kev's route. Kev comes back from the kitchen, finds his laptop on your desk again, blames himself, and carries it
home humming, right behind the auditor, who is glaring at a pen that will not write. Your desk has just been ticked with it; a few minutes later
Kev's desk is ticked with it too. The win picture: you smug behind an empty desk, the auditor beaming over four ticks,
and Kev with the laptop that was counted twice, thanks to him.
