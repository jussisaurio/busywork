# The Layoff List

## Card
"3:00 PM. At 3:45 the boss emails HR the layoff list. You're on it."
"Get your name off it."

## Name
The Layoff List is the thing on the boss's screen: a white sheet with a red title bar and a row of four faces, yours
framed in red. At 3:06, when Kev gets his computer working, the sheet pops out large beside his screen, your frame
throbbing, and you shudder (😨). After that it stays readable through the glass of his office from your own desk.
The name says what the danger is and where it is before the player has solved anything.

## The situation
- You are the office worker in the red sweater at the middle desk of the open floor.
- You want to keep your job.
- At 3:45 the boss clicks send, and the list goes to HR. Everyone on it is out. If your face is still on it, you
  carry your desk out in a cardboard box.
- HR gets whatever is on his screen at 3:45. Take your face off the sheet before then and it goes out without you.
- His computer is in his glass office. Lin sits facing that office, so nobody can touch his computer as
  themselves without being seen. But to someone who doesn't know better, people are their clothes.

## Cast
- **The boss**: bald, light blue shirt, red tie; his dark suit jacket hangs on the coat rack in the corner of his office.
  - 3:00: at his desk. His screen is static. He is angry at it (😠).
  - 3:01.4: he waves someone over. His bubble shows Kev's face and a wave.
  - 3:02 to 3:06: he gets up and stands to the side of his desk, arms out, watching Kev fix it.
  - 3:06: the static clears and the list comes up. He smiles and takes his chair back at about 3:07.
  - 3:12: a restroom break. He walks out across the floor and is out of sight from about 3:18.3. His computer is
    left on, list showing.
  - 3:26: he comes out of the restroom and walks back across the floor (about 3:26 to 3:32.5).
  - 3:35: the static comes back. 😠, then Kev's face and a wave, then a thought with Kev's face and a question mark.
    Kev went home, so nobody comes.
  - 3:37.5: nobody came. He thumps the desk and the screen works again.
  - 3:45: he clicks send.
- **Kev**: young, spiky hair, olive hoodie. The office's computer person. His desk is behind Lin's, by the entrance.
  - 3:01.6: called over by the boss. He walks into the office, says 🔧 at the boss's computer (about 3:04.2) and
    types at it until 3:06.
  - 3:06: the list comes up and he walks back to his desk.
  - About 3:08.7: back in his chair, hot (🥵, drops fly off him), he takes his hoodie off and hangs it over the
    back of his charcoal chair. From here he sits in a white T-shirt.
  - 3:19: he goes home early, while the boss is out of sight in the restroom. He picks up his bag, walks to the
    front of Lin's desk and says goodbye (a wave and a house, about 3:21.3). She waves back (about 3:22).
  - He walks back past his desk, the hoodie left on the chair, and out to the elevator. Gone at about 3:25.2.
  - The boss never sees him leave, so to the boss Kev is still here.
- **Lin**: dark hair, teal top. Her desk faces the boss's glass office, so she sees his computer and his coat rack.
  - 3:00 to 3:32: at her desk.
  - About 3:21: Kev says goodbye to her and she waves back, so she knows he went home.
  - 3:32: she goes to the restroom, once the boss is back at his desk. Out of sight from about 3:37.2.
  - 3:40: she comes back and walks to her desk, there by 3:45.

## Ways to win

### Why changing the list saves you
- The card says it: at 3:45 the boss emails HR the layoff list, and you are on it.
- At 3:06 the list pops out large beside his screen: a sheet with a red title bar and four faces, yours in a red
  frame that throbs. You shudder (😨). Then it shrinks back onto his monitor, readable from your desk through the
  glass.
- When you take your name off, the sheet pops out large again: your face is struck through and drops off it.
  Three faces remain, and stay.
- At 3:45 he clicks send: the sheet on his screen shrinks and flies off. The win cards show that flying sheet
  without your face; the lose cards show it with your face framed in red.

### Where the player sees that a disguise gets you to his computer
- 3:01.4 to 3:06: the boss calls Kev with Kev's face in his bubble. Kev walks in, says 🔧 and types at the boss's
  computer while the boss stands aside watching. The static clears and the boss smiles. This is the one time
  anyone but the boss touches that computer, and the boss is happy about it.
- As yourself, his computer only offers "take your name off the list", and only while he is away. Lin is facing
  you through the glass the whole time.
- When you wear Kev's hoodie and the boss sees you, he greets you with a smile and Kev's face: he takes you for Kev.
- When you wear the boss's jacket and Lin sees you walk to his computer, she shows a nervous face and the boss's
  face: she takes you for the boss.

### Route 1: as Kev (the hoodie)
Steps:
1. From 3:19, when Kev gets up to go, his hoodie hangs on his chair. Put it on at his desk ("put on Kev's hoodie").
   Do it while the boss is still out of sight (until about 3:26): if he sees you put it on, he knows it's you.
2. Wait for his computer to act up again at 3:35, and for Lin to leave for the restroom (3:32). She saw Kev go
   home.
3. While the static is on his screen (3:35 to 3:37.5), walk into the boss's office to his computer and "offer to
   fix his computer". You offer with a raised hand and a wrench (🙋🔧), as Kev did. The boss smiles back
   ("😊 🖥️"), gets up and stands aside to watch, as he did for Kev at 3:00. While his screen works there is
   nothing to fix, and his computer offers you nothing.
4. "Take your name off the list" (3 seconds) while he watches. The sheet pops out and your face drops off it. He
   thanks "Kev" (a smile and Kev's face) and sits back down.
5. Hang the hoodie back on Kev's chair ("leave his hoodie on his chair") without Lin seeing you in it (she is
   back at 3:40).
6. Be at your desk by 3:45, in your own sweater.

## Ways to lose
Every lose card draws you as you are when the day ends: in your sweater, or still in the hoodie or the jacket.
- **Do nothing, or get stopped.** At 3:45 the sheet flies off his screen with your face on it. Card: the sheet
  with your face in a red frame flies off. You walk out with your desk in a box, past your empty desk and chair.
  Ways to get stopped, each with the same card:
  - Lin or the boss sees you at his computer as yourself. They point at you and you are sent to your desk with
    work.
  - Lin sees you in Kev's hoodie. She saw him leave: a double take (Kev's face and a question mark), then she
    points at you.
  - Lin sees you put the jacket on. A double take (the boss's face and a question mark), then she points at you.
- **Caught.** The boss sees you in his jacket after 3:26. He stops, angry, and points at you. Card: the boss on
  his feet, pointing the way out. You leave with your box, still in his jacket, and your face is still on the
  list.
- **Blamed.** Your face came off, but at 3:45 your chair is empty. Card: your empty chair in a pool of light. The
  boss, angry, puts your face back on the list, and it flies off with you on it.
- **Blamed, as Kev.** Your face came off, but at 3:45 you sit at your desk still in Kev's hoodie. To the boss
  that is Kev in your chair, so you are not there. He stares (Kev's face and a question mark). Card: you at your
  desk in the hoodie; the boss, angry, puts your face back on the list, and it flies off with you on it. The same
  happens if Lin catches you in the hoodie after the edit: she points, you are sent to your desk, still in it.

## The win cards
- **Hoodie.** The boss at his desk, pleased, sends the list without you. Kev's empty chair has the hoodie back
  over it; you sit at your desk in your red sweater, smug.
- **Jacket.** The same, with the jacket back on the rack.

## Set dressing
The office is level 1's, with only what this level uses: no coffee pot and no fridge in the kitchen (in level 1
they hold things you can take; here nothing in them is yours to take). The plants and desk mugs stay.

## The joke
You, in Kev's olive hoodie, at the boss's keyboard, deleting yourself from the layoff list. The boss stands right
behind you, smiling and nodding along, glad his computer is in good hands. When you finish, he thanks "Kev".
