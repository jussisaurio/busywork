# Weekend Shift

## Card

Friday, 4:00 PM. At 5:00 the boss picks who works Saturday.
Make sure it isn't you.

## Name

"Weekend Shift": somebody in this office has to come in and work on Saturday, and you're the one down for it. The card says so before you move, and the whiteboard in the middle of the room shows it: your face in the box under Saturday.

## The situation

You're an office worker, 4:00 on a Friday. The Saturday roster is a whiteboard on a stand in the middle of the office: a strip of seven day boxes along the top, Saturday's in red, and a red line from it down to one big box with a face in it. At 4:00 that face is yours. At 5:00 the boss stands beside the board and reads it out, and whoever's face is in the box works Saturday.

If you fail, you spend a sunny Saturday alone in the office at a desk with a pile of work. Writing someone else into the box makes the boss read out their face instead, and they get the Saturday.

The board is in full view. The boss, at his desk behind the glass, faces it, and so does Lin at her desk. You can't write on it while either is looking. You have to make them look away.

## Cast

- **The boss** watches the office from his desk behind the glass wall, facing the board. He glances about every few seconds, but never long enough to write.
  - 4:20 to 4:25: he photocopies in the corner with his back to the room.
  - 4:50: he comes out to stand beside the board, ready for the 5:00 reading. From then on nobody writes on it.
- **Lin** sits at her desk in the bottom row, facing the board.
  - 4:00: her lunch box sits on her desk, a fish in a plastic tub.
  - 4:30: she stands, picks up the lunch box and carries it to the kitchen microwave. Her desk is empty while she's away. She stands in front of the microwave with her back to the room while her fish turns inside.
  - 4:37: she's back at her desk, happily eating it out of the open box.
- **Kev** sits at the far desk in the bottom row, too far off to see the board, his head down in his code. He's the one who fixes the machines: when the copier jams, a small ❗ pops up over his head, and he goes to fix it. He kneels at the copier with a spanner for about four game minutes.
- **4:55**: everyone gathers at the board for the reading. Your place between Lin and Kev is a dashed outline on the floor while you aren't in it.

## Ways to win

Both routes end the same way. Write a new face into Saturday's box while nobody is looking, then stand in front of the board at 5:00. Writing takes 2.5 seconds. Writing once puts the boss's face in the box. Writing again (the board still lights up) changes it to Kev's. Each time, the new face pops up big off the board for a beat, then settles into the box.

### The ding

Steps:

1. Heat your noodles in the microwave.
2. Walk to the board before the ding.
3. When it dings, write.
4. Stay near the board until 5:00.

Facts, and where you see each one:

- **The board decides, and you're on it.** 4:00, the opening frame: the board mid-room shows your face in the red box under the red Saturday box. The card says someone works Saturday.
- **You can write on it.** Any time: walk up to the board and it lights up, with the E key cap on its far side.
- **The boss and Lin are watching it.** From 4:00: their sight cones cover the board. The boss's is drawn strongest. Lin's comes up from her desk.
- **You can heat noodles in the microwave.** Any time: walk up to the microwave on the kitchen counter (east wall) and it lights up. From the moment you press it, its door is lit, the bowl turns inside, and big green digits on its display count down from 10.
- **The ding turns every head toward the kitchen.** 10 seconds after you start it, wherever you are: a ripple spreads from the microwave, every head and sight cone snaps east toward it, and they stay turned for about 6 seconds. Most players learn this the first time they heat their noodles, so the trick is a restart away.
- **The board is on the far side of the room from the microwave.** Seen in the opening frame: the board is on the west side, the microwave on the east wall. When the heads turn to the microwave, the board is behind them.
- **You must be at the board at 5:00.** 4:55: everyone gathers in front of the board and leaves one place for you, a dashed outline on the floor between Lin and Kev. The clock goes red at 4:50 and starts to tick.

### The phone

Steps:

1. Wait until about 4:30.
2. Step to the phone at the far end of your desk and ring the boss's phone.
3. Walk to the board.
4. Write while the boss is on the phone in his corner and Lin is at the microwave.
5. Stay near the board until 5:00.

Facts, and where you see each one:

- **Your desk has a phone that rings the boss's.** 4:00: a phone at the far end of your desk, and one on a little side table in the top-right corner of the boss's office, under his window. It is out of reach of your seat (nothing is lit on the first frame): stand up and step to it, and it lights up. Ringing draws a dotted line from your phone to his, his phone shakes, and the boss jumps.
- **The boss takes the call standing at that phone, with his back to the office, for about 5 game minutes.** Right after the ring: he walks to the corner and stands beside the table, facing the window. The handset is gone from the base, and a coiled cord runs from the base to the handset at his ear, so it's plainly that phone. Nobody is on the line, and he hangs up angry.
- **Lin still watches the board from her desk.** Her cone still covers the board while the boss is on the phone. Write then and she points at you, and the boss sends you back to your desk to type for 20 minutes. You see the papers fly. The phone is one step from your seat at 4:00, so ringing straight away is the obvious first try, and this is how most players learn that Lin watches.
- **Lin leaves at 4:30 for the microwave.** 4:30: she gets up with her lunch box and walks to the kitchen, leaving her desk bare. 4:34: she stands at the microwave with her back to the room while a fish turns in it. 4:37: she's back at her desk with it.
- **So ring at about 4:30.** The boss is on the phone in his corner from about 4:33 to 4:38 and Lin is at the microwave from about 4:34 to 4:37. Both are facing away from the board, and you can walk from your desk and write.

### The trap (not a win)

The copier. Jam a paper clip in it: a crumpled sheet sticks out of its top, its light flashes red, and it beeps every 2 seconds until Kev has fixed it, the beeps drawn coming off it. Every head turns toward it. But the copier is in the corner behind the board, so the board, and you at it, are in plain view.

## Ways to lose

- **Fail: your face is still on the board at 5:00.** On the map: just before 5:00 the boss turns to the board, announces it, and its Saturday box glows red. Then he points at you. End card: Saturday, the sun in the window, the office empty except for you at a desk with a pile of work. Your face is on the board beside you.
- **Caught: you wrote while someone was looking.** On the map: whoever saw you raises the alarm, and the boss stops you (❗😠) and the screen shakes. End card: you at the desk on Saturday, and the boss standing by the board, angry, pointing at you.
- **Told on: Lin saw you while the boss was away.** She points at you (❗👉) and tells the boss. He sends you back to your desk to type for 20 game minutes, papers flying, and the board is unchanged. This is not an ending. If there's time, you can try again.
- **Blamed: you changed the board but weren't in front of it at 5:00.** Whoever isn't here gets Saturday. On the map: your place in the half-circle is outlined red on the floor, every head turns to it and wonders where you are (your face, ❓), then points it out (👉 your face). End card: Friday at 5:00, round the board. It still shows the face you wrote, but the boss points at your empty, outlined place, and Lin and Kev have turned to look at it.

## The joke

5:00, everyone round the board. The boss turns to it to read out who works Saturday, finds his own face in the box and goes red at the board. Then the card: a sunny Saturday, the office empty, and the boss alone at a desk, typing.
