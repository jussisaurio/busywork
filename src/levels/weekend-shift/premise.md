# Weekend Shift

## Card

Friday, 4:00 PM. You're down for Saturday.
At 5:00 the boss reads it out.

## Name

"Weekend Shift": somebody in this office has to come in and work on Saturday, and the board already says it's you. The card says so before you move, and the whiteboard in the middle of the room shows it: your face in the box under Saturday.

## The situation

You're an office worker, 4:00 on a Friday. The Saturday roster is a whiteboard on a stand in the middle of the office: a strip of seven day boxes along the top, Saturday's in red, and a red line from it down to one big box with a face in it. At 4:00 that face is yours. Nobody picks at 5:00: the board has already picked. At 5:00 the boss reads it out, and whoever's face is in the box works Saturday.

If you fail, you spend a sunny Saturday alone in the office at a desk with a pile of work. Writing someone else into the box makes the boss read out their face instead, and they get the Saturday.

The board is in full view. The boss, at his desk behind the glass, faces it, and so does Lin at her desk. You can't write on it while either is looking. You have to pull their eyes away from the board.

## Cast

- **The boss** watches the office from his desk behind the glass wall, facing the board. He glances about every few seconds, but never long enough to write. His desk has a folder on it and no phone: his phone stands on a little table in the top-right corner of his office, under the window.
  - 4:20 to 4:25: he photocopies at the copier with his back to the room. If it's jammed, he's cross about it.
  - 4:50: he comes out, takes the black marker from the board's tray, and stands beside the board with his back to it, facing the room. From then on nobody writes on it under his nose. At 4:55 he sits down there.
- **Lin** sits at her desk in the bottom row, facing the board.
  - 4:00: her lunch box sits on her desk, a fish in a plastic tub.
  - 4:30: she stands, picks up the lunch box and carries it to the kitchen. Her desk is empty while she's away. She waits a step back from the microwave, facing it, her back to the room and her cone pointing east, away from the board, while her fish turns inside.
  - 4:37: she's back at her desk, happily eating it out of the open box. Kev, two desks down, looks over at it: 🤢.
- **Kev** sits at the far desk in the bottom row, too far off to see the board, his head down in his code. He's the one who fixes the machines: when the copier jams he turns to it with a ❗, a dashed line from him to it, walks over, and kneels at its open front with a spanner (🔧) for about four game minutes. Its light goes amber while he works and green when he's done.
- **4:55**: everyone gathers at the board for the reading: the boss on a chair beside it, Lin and Kev on chairs facing it, with an empty chair between them. That's yours. If you aren't in the meeting area when they sit down, each looks at your empty chair with a ❓.

## Ways to win

Both routes end the same way. Write a new face into Saturday's box while nobody is looking, then be at the board at 5:00. Writing takes 2.5 seconds and is drawn: the black marker leaves the tray, and the new face is scribbled over yours line by line, from the top. Writing once puts the boss's face in the box. Writing again (the board still lights up) changes it to Kev's. Each time, the new face pops up big off the board for a beat in a soft white ring, then settles into the box.

### The ding

Steps:

1. Heat your noodles in the microwave.
2. Walk to the board before the ding.
3. When it dings, write.
4. Stay near the board until 5:00.

Facts, and where you see each one:

- **The board decides, and you're on it.** 4:00, the opening frame: the board mid-room shows your face in the red box under the red Saturday box. The card says you're down for Saturday.
- **You can write on it.** Any time: walk up to the board and it lights up, with the E key cap on its far side. Two markers sit in its tray.
- **The boss and Lin are watching it.** From 4:00: their sight cones cover the board. The boss's is drawn strongest. Lin's comes up from her desk.
- **You can heat noodles in the microwave.** Any time: a cup of instant noodles stands on the kitchen counter under the microwave (east wall). Walk up and the microwave lights up. From the moment you press it, the cup is gone from the counter: it turns in the lit window, steaming, and big green digits on the display count down from 10.
- **The ding turns every head toward the kitchen.** 10 seconds after you start it, wherever you are: the display reads 00, a ripple spreads from the microwave, every head and sight cone snaps east toward it, and they stay turned for about 6 seconds. Most players learn this the first time they heat their noodles, so the trick is a restart away.
- **The board is on the far side of the room from the microwave.** Seen in the opening frame: the board is on the west side, the microwave on the east wall. When the heads turn to the microwave, the board is behind them.
- **You must be at the board at 5:00.** 4:55: everyone sits down round the board, leaving the chair between Lin and Kev empty, and if you're elsewhere they look at it (❓). The clock goes red at 4:50 and starts to tick.

### The phone

Steps:

1. Wait until about 4:30.
2. Go into the supply room (top left, behind a wall) and ring the boss's phone from the phone on its little table.
3. Walk to the board.
4. Write while the boss is on the phone in his corner and Lin is at the microwave.
5. Stay near the board until 5:00.

Facts, and where you see each one:

- **There's a phone that rings the boss's, out of his sight.** 4:00: a phone on a little table in the supply room, and one on a little table in the top-right corner of the boss's office; his desk has none, so that one is his. The supply room is behind a solid wall, so the boss can't see you at its phone. It is far from your seat (nothing is lit on the first frame): walk in and it lights up. Ringing lifts your handset off its base, draws a dotted line from your phone to his, shakes his phone, and the boss jumps (☎️❗).
- **The boss takes the call standing at that phone, with his back to the office, for about 5 game minutes.** Right after the ring: he walks to the corner and stands beside the table, facing the window. His handset is at his ear on a coiled cord from the base, so it's plainly that phone. Nobody is on the line (❓), and he hangs up angry (😠). Your handset stays off its base and the dotted line stays live the whole call: it's your call.
- **Lin still watches the board from her desk.** Her cone still covers the board while the boss is on the phone. Write then and she points at you, and the boss sends you back to your desk to type for 20 minutes. You see the papers fly. Ringing straight away is the obvious first try, and this is how most players learn that Lin watches.
- **Lin leaves at 4:30 for the microwave.** 4:30: she gets up with her lunch box and walks to the kitchen, leaving her desk bare. About 4:32 to 4:37: she waits at the microwave with her back to the room while a fish turns in it. 4:37: she's back at her desk with it.
- **So ring at about 4:30.** The walk to the supply room takes a few seconds, and the boss needs about 2 game minutes to reach his corner. Ring between about 4:27 and 4:32, and both are facing away from the board long enough for you to walk from the supply room to the board and write.

### The trap (not a win)

The copier, below-left of the board. Jam a paper clip in it: a crumpled sheet sticks out of its top, its light flashes red, and it beeps every 2 seconds until Kev has fixed it, red arcs coming off it on every beep. Every head turns toward it. But the copier is lined up behind the board as the boss sees it from his desk: his cone, turned to the copier, crosses the board, and you at it are in plain view. Distractions pull eyes toward something; this one pulls them through the board.

## Ways to lose

- **Fail: your face is still on the board at 5:00.** On the map: just before 5:00 the boss announces it to the room (📢), turns to the board and points the marker at it (👈 your face), and the Saturday box glows warm round your face. He keeps it there until 5:00. End card: Saturday, the sun in the window, the office empty except for you at a desk with a pile of work. Your face is on the board beside you.
- **Caught: you wrote while the boss was looking.** On the map: the boss stops you (❗😠) and the screen shakes. The face you were halfway through scribbling stays on the board, the marker at it. End card: you at the desk on Saturday, and the boss standing by the board, angry, pointing at you; the board still shows the half-drawn face over yours.
- **Told on: Lin saw you while the boss was away.** She points at you (❗👉) and tells the boss. He sends you back to your desk to type for 20 game minutes, papers flying, and the board is unchanged. This is not an ending. If there's time, you can try again.
- **Blamed: you changed the board but weren't at it at 5:00.** Whoever isn't there gets Saturday. On the map: the boss reads out the face you wrote, but your chair is empty. End card: Friday at 5:00, round the board. It still shows the face you wrote, but the boss points at your empty place, outlined red on the floor, and Lin and Kev have turned to look at it.

## The joke

From 4:50 the boss sits with his back to the board, holding its marker, sure of what it says. At 5:00 he announces it, turns, points the marker at it, and finds his own face in the box. He goes red, and Lin and Kev clap at once (👏), so he's stuck with it in front of everyone. Then the card: a sunny Saturday, the office empty, and the boss alone at a desk, typing, his face on the board beside him.
