# Second Thoughts

## Card

3:00 PM. Your angry resignation letter is in the boss's tray.
He goes through it at 3:40.

## Name

"Second Thoughts": you wrote the boss an angry resignation letter, left it in his tray, and now you regret it. The name says what you are in trouble for. It says nothing about how you get out of it.

## The situation

You're an office worker. At lunch you lost your temper, wrote an angry resignation letter and dropped it in the boss's in-tray. It's 3:00 and you've calmed down. You want to keep your job.

The tray is a wire basket on his desk in his glass office. The office has two doors: one onto the hallway, and a side door into the meeting room, right beside the tray. Your white envelope lies in it, and you can see it there from the start. At 3:40 he goes through his tray, as he does every day. If the letter is still there, he reads it, and you're packing a cardboard box.

If you take it back first, there's nothing for him to read. He never knew it existed, so nobody can miss it. Where you are at 3:40 doesn't matter, as long as he hasn't seen you with it.

The trouble is that he sits facing his tray all afternoon, and in the light nothing on his desk escapes him. Taking the letter takes 2.5 seconds, longer than any glance. The level's one rule is that the dark shrinks every view to arm's length, except along the beam of a phone torch.

## Cast

- **The boss** sits at his desk facing his tray, from 3:00 until 3:40. He never gets up on his own, and he does no work: he dreads the tray. On a fixed five-minute round from 3:01, the same every loop, he looks at it and sighs (📥😮‍💨, a shrug), drums his fingers on the desk, and checks the brass alarm clock at the end of his desk (⏰😮‍💨, a glint off its face). None of it moves his eyes off the desk.
  - 3:39: the desk clock rings. He sighs and drags the tray over.
  - If his office goes dark (his light switch, in the corner behind his back), he says ❓ and holds up his phone as a torch. As he gets up, his beam swings across the east half of his office; then he walks it over to the switch and flips it back, annoyed. The tubes blink a few times before they catch, about three minutes after the flip. Whoever his torch finds in his dark office is the one who flipped it.
  - If the whole floor goes dark (the fuse), he stays put and holds up his phone as a torch, pointed at his hallway door (waiting for someone to come and fix it). The beam lights the front of his desk and that door, the same every loop. The side of his desk and the side door stay dark.
  - 3:40: he goes through his tray, there at his desk. If your letter is in it, he has already seen it: ✉️😠.
- **Lin** sits at her desk on the open floor.
  - 3:05: she goes to the kitchen for her afternoon coffee and switches on the old coffee machine at about 3:10. Left alone, the old machine only makes the lights flicker all over the floor: she's startled, Kev looks down the hall at the fuse box, the fuse holds and the lights steady. Three minutes later it has brewed: green lamp, full pot. A full pot pours a cup, for you too; the cup leads nowhere.
  - If someone tipped the water jug over it first, it shorts: sparks and smoke, and a spark runs down the wiring to the fuse box and trips it for the whole floor. A shorted machine brews nothing. If the power is already out, nothing happens and she's puzzled.
  - 3:23: she pours herself a cup if it brewed, and drinks it at the kitchen table. 3:31: back at her desk, her cup beside her.
- **Kev** sits at the far desk on the open floor. He's the one who resets fuses. From his chair he sees through the kitchen door to the front of the coffee machine.
  - Whenever the power goes out, he holds up his phone and walks down the hallway to the fuse box. He resets it, which takes about three minutes, and the lights come back on. A blackout lasts about eight minutes in all.
  - If he finds someone standing at the fuse box, he takes them for the one who pulled it and tells the boss. You're sent to your desk to do assigned work for 20 minutes.

## Ways to win

Every route is the same last step: take the letter from the tray while it's too dark for the boss to see you, then keep it out of his sight once the light is back. Walk well away from his glass office (your own desk is far enough), or eat it.

### The switch

Steps:

1. Walk into his office to the light switch in the corner behind his back (pretend to be busy on the way) and flip it.
2. Get out of his torch's way: out of the east half of the office while he gets up, and away from the switch while he walks to it.
3. Take the letter from his tray while he is at the switch, and get away from his office before his light comes back on.

Facts, and where you see each one:

- **Your letter is in his tray, and he's watching it.** 3:00, the opening frame: the white envelope lies in the wire basket on his desk, he sits facing it, and his sight cone covers the whole desk.
- **Taking it in the light gets you caught.** Try it: he points at you, and the end card shows him pointing at you from his chair as you reach for his tray.
- **His office has its own light switch.** From 3:00: a white wall switch in the north-west corner of his office, behind his back. Stand by it and a prompt appears.
- **Only his office goes dark, and in the dark he can't see past arm's length.** The moment you flip it: his office turns navy, the rest of the floor stays lit, the switch shows an amber lamp, and his sight shrinks to arm's length, except along the beam of the phone he holds up.
- **He leaves his desk to fix it, and his torch looks for you.** Right after the flip: a puzzled thought over his head and a phone flash in his hand. As he gets up, his beam swings across the east half of the office; then he walks it to the switch, his hand goes to it, and the tubes blink back on about three minutes after the flip.
- **Don't be in his beam.** Whoever his torch finds in his dark office flipped it, pretending or not. The end card shows his torch on you, the office dark, your letter where it was.
- **Don't let him see you with it.** If he sees the envelope in your hand once the light is back, he catches you. The end card shows him pointing at you holding it.

### The blackout

Steps:

1. Before 3:10, walk into the kitchen and tip the water jug over the coffee machine, from beside it (by the fridge), where Kev can't see you. Pretend to be busy on the way.
2. At about 3:10 Lin switches it on: it shorts, and the whole floor goes dark.
3. Walk to the side of his desk, by the side door, outside his torch beam, and take the letter.
4. Leave by the side door, and get away from his office before Kev turns the power back on.

Facts, and where you see each one:

- **The old coffee machine strains the wiring.** Any first loop, just by waiting. 3:05: Lin walks to the coffee machine on the kitchen counter. At about 3:10 she switches it on and the lights all over the floor flicker. She's startled, and Kev, at his desk, turns to look down the hall at the fuse box. The lights steady, and the machine brews.
- **A full water jug stands beside it, and you can tip it.** From 3:00: a glass jug of water on the counter beside the machine. Stand by the machine and a prompt appears. Water runs down the machine's front into a puddle, and the jug lies on its side from then on.
- **Kev watches the front of the machine.** From his desk, his sight cone reaches through the kitchen door to the front of the machine. Tip the jug from there and he sees you, points you out to the boss, and you're sent to your desk to work. From beside the machine, by the fridge, he can't see you.
- **Soaked, it shorts and the whole floor goes dark.** At about 3:10: sparks and a burst of grey smoke, Lin startled, and a spark runs out of the kitchen and down the wiring to the fuse box at the end of the hallway. Its door flies open, a red lamp blinks, and the office, the meeting room, the kitchen and the open floor all go dark. Only the strip of floor by the glass lobby keeps its daylight. The machine stays scorched black, smoking while the power is out.
- **In the dark, the boss only sees where his phone points.** From the blackout: everyone holds up a phone with its flash lit, and each throws a lit beam on the floor. His beam rests on his hallway door, across the front of his desk. The side of his desk, beside the tray, and the side door stay dark.
- **The dark lasts about eight minutes.** Right after it: Kev walks his torch down the hallway to the fuse box. About eight minutes later the lights come back.

### The breaker

Steps:

1. Walk to the fuse box at the west end of the hallway and pull the main breaker. Any time works.
2. Get out of the hallway at once, walk to the side of his desk outside his beam, and take the letter.
3. Get away from his office before the lights come back.

Facts, and where you see each one:

- **The fuse box runs the floor's lights.** From 3:00: a grey metal box with a yellow high-voltage sticker on the end wall of the hallway, door shut, green lamp. At 3:10, when the lights flicker, Kev looks straight at it.
- **You can pull it yourself.** Stand at it and a prompt appears. Pulling it puts out every light on the floor. Its door hangs open and its lamp blinks red.
- **The boss can't see the fuse box from his chair.** The opening frame: it's around the corner in the hallway, behind the solid south-west wall of his office, outside his sight cone.
- **Kev comes to the fuse box, so don't be there.** A second or so after the lights go out, Kev walks his torch down the hallway to it. If you're still there when he arrives, he tells the boss, and you're sent to your desk to work.

## Ways to lose

- **3:40 and the letter is still in his tray** (or he picked it up after you dropped it where he could see it). The end card: he holds your envelope up over his desk, furious, his tray empty, and you stand sweating in front of it; the card's 📦 says the rest.
- **Caught with it.** You reached for it while he could see you, or he saw it in your hand. The end card: his light on, he points at you from his chair, and you stand where he saw you, reaching for the tray or with the envelope in your hand.
- **Found in his dark office.** You flipped his light and his torch found you. The end card: his office dark, his phone torch on you where you stood, and your letter still in his tray (or in your hand, if you'd taken it).
- **Found at the fuse box.** Kev arrives and finds you standing at it, and tells the boss. You lose 20 minutes at your desk doing assigned work. That usually means the 3:40 card above.
- **Seen soaking the coffee machine.** Kev sees you tip the jug from the front of the machine, and points you out to the boss: 20 minutes of assigned work at your desk, and the machine stays dry.

## The joke

The blackout win. The room is dark. The boss sits at his desk with his phone torch trained on his door, guarding his tray, and the tray is empty. Just outside his beam you slip out of the side door, smug, with the envelope in your hand. In the kitchen, in Lin's torchlight, her coffee machine is still smoking in a puddle.

## End cards

Every card shows the day as it ended (3:40 for the 3:40 cards, the moment you were caught otherwise), drawn from the state: what you hold, where each person is, the lights. A win: the lights on, the boss at his desk holding up his empty tray, baffled (❓), and you at your desk (or wherever you are) with whatever you hold. Behind, the route's aftermath: the coffee machine you soaked, scorched, its jug on its side in a puddle, after the blackout; the fuse box after the breaker.
