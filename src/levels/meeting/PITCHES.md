# Meeting: pitches

Tick what is worth building (`- [x]`), and add a word if you like. Only ticked pitches get built (`docs/PLAN.md`).
Three pitchers, each with a different slant, worked independently; the lead merged their work, removed duplicates and cut it
down. The last group comes from a blind play through the harness (an agent that had not read the level). The order is by group, not by preference.

Format: what you do → what you see → the punchline. Cost: **cheap** = a prop, a bubble, an end scene or a condition on
state that exists. **engine** = needs a new engine seam (named). Props that do not exist yet are marked *new prop*.
The office today has: wall clock, potted plants, framed pictures, typewriters, the coat rack, the fridge, the coffee
machine, the projector, the elevator and the fire door. It has no phone, no whiteboard, no microwave and no bell.
