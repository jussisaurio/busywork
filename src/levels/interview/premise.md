# The Interview

## Card
3:00 PM. Lin wants your job. Her phone interview is at 3:30.
She must miss it.

## Name
"The Interview": at 3:30 head office phones Lin to interview her for your job. That call is the trouble. If she takes
it, the job is hers. The name says what is coming and when. It says nothing about the switchboard, the hold music or
the plug in the wall, which are the ways out.

## The situation
You work in an open-plan office. Lin, who sits below the boss's glass wall, wants your job, and head office
interviews her for it by phone at 3:30. The call comes in on the office's outside line and rings her desk phone. If
she answers it at her desk, she gets your job. The end card shows her on the phone in your chair, at your desk with
your nameplate and your red mug, her own desk empty behind her, while you stand by with your things in a box.

The call has to fail. It fails when somebody else answers it, when her line is busy, when her phone is dead, or when
nobody answers it at all. Whatever happens, be back at your desk by the end of the call (3:34). From 3:30 the boss
keeps glancing at your chair (and if it is empty: your face, ?). An empty chair while your job is being decided looks
bad, even if the call failed. The day is a time loop: when you lose, it is 3:00 again.

## Cast
- **The boss** (navy suit, glass office top left). He sits at his desk all afternoon. He faces the open plan through
  his glass wall, so his sight cone covers Lin's desk and the wall socket beside it.
  - He loves hold music. He stays on for 9 minutes, whichever phone he picks up. His own phone he takes standing at
    his window, his back to the room and his eyes on the view; on another phone he stares ahead. The tune visibly
    ends: the notes stop, he smiles (smile) and hangs up. On hold nobody glances round, though the 3:30 ring turns
    his head (!) for a moment: the tune keeps him.
  - When Lin's phone rings and somebody else goes to it, he half rises from his chair (!) and sits back down.
  - At 3:30 the call is not his, so he stays at his desk. If Lin's phone rings and she is not there to take it, he is
    the nearest person who can hear it.
- **Lin** (teal cardigan, the desk below the boss's glass wall). Her phone is teal. Its power cable, a coiled black
  cable, runs down to a double socket in the wall beside her desk; its plug sits in the top outlet.
  - 3:00 to 3:04: she waits by her phone and checks her line, at about 3:00 and again at about 3:03: she lifts the
    receiver, listens, puts it back and looks up at the wall clock (clock thought). If the line is dead (her plug
    pulled), she says ? and pushes the plug back into the wall herself.
  - 3:04: nerves. She goes to the kitchen and sits at the table with a tea from about 3:08.
  - 3:22: she goes back to her desk for the call with her notes. She sits down at about 3:26 and glances at the clock
    now and then.
  - She keeps her own line free for the interview. If her own phone rings before 3:30, she picks up (?), hears the
    hold music and slams the receiver down (angry). The kitchen's red wall phone is on the outside line, like her
    interview: on it she hopes it is her interviewer, trying to reach her there (briefcase, ?), and stays on hold for
    10 minutes. On the boss's or Kev's phone she hangs up at once (shrug).
- **Kev** (olive hoodie, the desk on the right). His phone is olive.
  - 3:00: coffee at the kitchen machine.
  - About 3:06: he ambles across the office with his mobile to his charging spot, the double socket by Lin's desk
    (about 3:12). He crouches and plugs his charger into the bottom outlet, under her plug (battery thought). If her
    plug lies on the floor, it is in his way: !, he jams it back into the top outlet with a spark, cross.
  - About 3:13: he takes his mobile back to the coffee machine (from about 3:19).
  - 3:27: back to his desk.
  - On any phone he holds for 4 minutes, then shrugs and hangs up.
- **You** (red jumper, the middle desk with the red mug).
  - **The switchboard.** The office's old cord switchboard stands in the empty reception nook above your desk. Each
    jack has a card over it with a band and a lamp in its line's colour, and patch cables hang below. One card shows a
    briefcase: the outside line, which you cannot use; its dark red lamp is there from the start. The others show
    Lin's face (teal), the boss's face (blue), Kev's face (olive) and a small red wall phone (the kitchen's, on the
    outside line). Plug into a jack and that phone rings: its lamp lights, and the phone sends out rings in its line's
    colour. The rings show how far the ring carries. The owner comes to answer if he is free and can hear it,
    hurrying (!). If not, the nearest free person who can hear it answers. A ring that nobody hears stops after a
    minute and a half. Whoever answers gets hold music (notes out of the receiver) and stays on, staring ahead, until
    he gives up (the times above). The lamp stays lit while the line is in use.
  - **The plug.** You can crouch at the socket by Lin's desk and pull her phone's plug out of the wall (it takes a
    moment). The cable then lies on the floor, its plug out, and her phone is dead. You cannot do this while her phone
    is ringing or in use.
  - Both are sneaky. If the boss sees you at either, you are caught. If Lin or Kev sees you, they shout and point
    (!, finger), and the boss sends you to your desk with 20 minutes of work.
  - Away from your desk, the boss also grows suspicious if he sees you standing about, and sends you back to it with
    20 minutes of work. Pretending to be busy (shift) on the way keeps him off you.

## Ways to win
You win when the 3:30 call fails and you are at your desk at 3:34. Each route has its own end card and route icon.

### 1. Someone else answers (route icon: shrug)
Steps:
1. Between about 3:16 and 3:21, while Lin is at her tea and Kev near the coffee machine, ring Kev's phone. Kev hears
   it from the kitchen and goes to his desk to answer it.
2. At once ring the kitchen phone. Kev is busy, so Lin, the free person nearest it, gets up from her tea and answers
   it. She holds for 10 minutes, past 3:30.
3. Go back to your desk.

At 3:30 Lin's desk phone rings red. Lin is still on hold in the kitchen. The boss starts (!), walks out to her phone
and answers it, puzzled (briefcase, ?). He asks after Lin (her face, ?), shrugs and hangs up. Lin has missed her call.
The end card: the boss at her desk with her handset, puzzled, and Lin back from the kitchen, too late and cross.

The facts, and when you see them:
- **Lin waits for a call at her desk phone.** At about 3:00 and 3:03 she lifts her receiver, listens and looks up at
  the clock (shots `start`, `lin-checks-the-clock`).
- **The call comes at 3:30 on the outside line and rings her desk phone.** The card gives the time. On the first loop
  at 3:30 you see the briefcase jack's red lamp light, her phone ring red and Lin answer it, delighted (shot
  `the-call`).
- **The switchboard rings phones.** Up close it lights up to show you can use it: a row of jacks under face cards
  (shot `switchboard`). Plug into one and that phone rings in its line's colour, its lamp lit (shot
  `kev-phone-rings`).
- **The owner walks over, answers and stays on.** Kev hears his phone at the coffee machine (!), walks to his desk
  and stands there with the receiver and the hold music (shots `kev-phone-rings`, `kev-on-hold`).
- **A ring goes to the nearest free person who hears it.** With Kev on his way to his own phone, Lin answers the
  kitchen phone (shot `lin-takes-the-kitchen-phone`). Ring the kitchen alone and Kev, nearer, takes it, so she is
  free again at 3:30 (the teaching fail).
- **Lin holds on, hoping it is her interviewer.** The kitchen's wall phone is red, the outside line's colour, like
  her interview. On it she says briefcase, ? and stays on (shot `lin-takes-the-kitchen-phone`). She is still there
  at 3:30 (shot `the-call-answered`).
- **Lin is away from 3:04 to 3:26.** She has her tea at the kitchen table (shot `lin-at-her-tea`).
- **The boss takes Lin's phone when she is away.** At 3:30 he starts and gets up to answer it (shot
  `the-call-answered`).

### 2. Her line is busy (route icon: notes)
Steps:
1. Between about 3:17 and 3:24, while Lin is away from her desk, ring Lin's phone. Not at about 3:23, when she walks
   past the switchboard on her way back: she would see you and tell on you.
2. Go back to your desk.

The boss hears it (!), hurries out of his office to her desk and answers it. He likes the hold music and stays on her
line for 9 minutes. Lin comes back at about 3:26 and finds him on her phone. At 3:30 her interviewer gets a busy
line. Lin glares at the boss (his face, angry).

The facts, and when you see them:
- **The boss answers Lin's phone when she is away, and stays on.** Shot `boss-on-lins-phone`: her teal phone rings
  on her empty desk and the boss comes out of his door to it. Shot `the-call-busy`: he is still on it at 3:30.
- **While Lin sits at her phone, she slams it down.** You see her pick up, hear the tune and slam the receiver down,
  angry (shot `lin-hangs-up-on-her-own-phone`).
- **Too early, and the boss is off her line again before 3:30.** He stays on for 9 minutes. Ring before about 3:17
  and you see him hang up and walk back to his office before the call comes.
- **A busy line means no call.** At 3:30 the boss is still on her line and Lin glares at him (shot
  `the-call-busy`).

### 3. Her phone is dead (route icon: plug)
Steps:
1. Between about 3:11 and 3:20, once Kev has been to the socket to charge his mobile (he leaves it at about 3:13),
   ring the boss's phone. He stands at his window with his back to the room for 9 minutes.
2. While he is on hold (and Lin is at her tea), go to the socket by Lin's desk and pull her plug out.
3. Go back to your desk.

At 3:30 nothing rings. Lin sits at her silent phone and wonders why her interviewer is late (clock, ?).

A variant: keep Kev from his charging stop. Ring Kev's phone at about 3:01. He is on hold at his desk until about
3:10, sets off for the socket, and at 3:13 turns back for more coffee without getting there. Ring the boss too, and
unplug once Lin has left at 3:04.

The facts, and when you see them:
- **Her phone needs its plug.** From the start you see the coiled cable run from her phone to a socket in the wall
  beside her desk, its plug in (shot `start`). After you pull it, the cable lies on the floor, its plug out, and the
  socket's top outlet is empty (shot `cord-pulled`).
- **The boss watches the socket.** His sight cone goes through the glass over Lin's desk and the socket (shot
  `start`). If you pull the plug while he looks, he points at you (shot `end-caught`).
- **On his own phone the boss turns his back.** He stands at the window with the receiver, his sight cone pointing
  out of the window (shot `boss-on-hold`).
- **Kev charges his mobile at that socket at about 3:12.** Shot `kev-at-the-socket`: Kev crouches at it and plugs
  his charger in under her plug (battery thought). If her plug is out, it lies in his way: !, he jams it back in with
  a spark (shot `kev-plugs-it-back`).
- **A dead phone means no call.** At 3:30 Lin waits at her silent phone with its cable on the floor (shot
  `the-call-dead`).

### 4. Nobody answers (route icon: bell)
Steps:
1. At about 3:18 ring Kev's phone, then the kitchen phone, as in route 1. Lin is on hold in the kitchen past 3:30.
2. Between about 3:22 and 3:29, ring the boss's phone. He is on hold at his window until after 3:32.
3. Go back to your desk.

At 3:30 Lin's phone rings red at her empty desk. Lin is on hold in the kitchen and the boss at his window: he turns
his head to the ring (!) but stays with his tune. Kev is back at his desk across the office, too far away to hear it.
It rings for two minutes and stops. The end card shows the same: the boss and Lin on hold, Kev at his desk.

The facts, and when you see them:
- Everything in route 1, plus: **the boss is the one who answers Lin's phone at 3:30**, and he cannot while he is on
  his own phone (shots `the-call-answered`, `boss-on-hold`).
- **He is on the phone for 9 minutes.** Ring him at 3:25 and he is still on when the ringing stops at 3:32. Ring him
  before about 3:22 and he hangs up in time to answer it (route 1 again). Ring him alone at 3:25 and Lin is back at
  her desk to take her call (the teaching fail).
- **Nobody comes.** Shot `the-call-rings-out`: the red rings at her empty desk, Lin on hold in the kitchen, the boss
  on hold at his window turning his head to the ring (!), Kev at his far desk.

## Ways to lose
- **Lin takes her call.** The end card: Lin in your chair, at your desk with your nameplate and your red mug, on the
  phone, beaming (briefcase, smile); her own desk stands empty behind. You stand by with your things in a box (shot
  `end-fail`).
- **Caught.** The boss sees you at the socket. The end card: you at the wall with Lin's plug in your fist, its cord
  running back to her phone, and the boss pointing at you, angry (shot `end-caught`).
- **Away from your desk.** The call failed, but at 3:34 your chair is empty. The end card: the boss points at your
  empty chair and desk with your red mug, while you hurry in late (shot `end-blamed`).
- **Told on.** Not a loss by itself. Lin or Kev sees you at the switchboard or the socket and points. The boss sends
  you to your desk with 20 minutes of work, and usually the window has closed when you are free.
- The teaching fails: each picture shows why. Ring only the kitchen and Kev answers it (Lin is free at 3:30). Ring
  Lin's phone while she sits at it and she slams it down. Pull the plug before Kev's charging stop and he jams it
  back in. Ring
  only the boss and Lin is at her desk at 3:30. Each ends with Lin taking her call.

## The joke
3:26 to 3:30 on the busy route. Lin hurries back from her tea for the call that will get her your job, and finds the
boss at her desk with her phone at his ear, eyes half shut, enjoying the hold music. He does not budge. At 3:30 her
interviewer gets a busy line, and Lin, beside him, glares at her own boss.
