# The Interview

## Card
3:00 PM. Lin wants your job. Her phone interview is at 3:30.
She must miss it.

## Name
"The Interview": at 3:30 head office phones Lin to interview her for your job. That call is the trouble. If she takes
it, the job is hers. The name says what is coming and when. It says nothing about the switchboard, the hold music or
the phone cord, which are the ways out.

## The situation
You work in an open-plan office. Head office is filling your position, and Lin, who sits below the boss's glass wall,
has applied. They interview her by phone at 3:30. The call comes in on the office's outside line and rings her desk
phone. If she answers it at her desk, she gets your job. The end card shows her on the phone in your chair, at your
desk with your red mug, while you stand by with your things in a box.

The call has to fail. It fails when somebody else answers it, when her line is busy, when her phone is dead, or when
nobody answers it at all. Whatever happens, be back at your desk by the end of the call (3:34). An empty chair while
your job is being decided looks bad, even if the call failed. The day is a time loop: when you lose, it is 3:00 again.

## Cast
- **The boss** (navy suit, glass office top left). He sits at his desk all afternoon. He faces the open plan through
  his glass wall, so his sight cone covers Lin's desk and the wall socket beside it.
  - He loves hold music. His own phone he takes standing at his window, his back to the room and his eyes on the view.
    He stays on for 7 minutes. On another phone he stays on for 9 minutes (Lin's) or 4 minutes (Kev's, the kitchen's),
    staring ahead. On hold nobody glances round.
  - At 3:30 the call is not his, so he stays at his desk. If Lin's phone rings and she is not there to take it, he is
    the nearest person who can hear it.
- **Lin** (teal cardigan, the desk below the boss's glass wall). Her phone is teal. Its cord runs to a socket in the
  wall beside her desk.
  - 3:00 to 3:04: she waits by her phone. At about 3:01 she lifts the receiver, listens to check that it works, puts it
    back and looks up at the wall clock (clock thought). She is waiting for a call.
  - 3:04: nerves. She goes to the kitchen and sits at the table with a tea from about 3:08.
  - 3:22: she goes back to her desk for the call. She sits down at about 3:26.
  - She keeps her own line free for the interview. If her own phone rings before 3:30, she picks up, hears nothing
    for her, and hangs up at once (?, angry). On any other phone she hopes it is her interviewer, trying to reach her
    there (briefcase, ?). She stays on hold for 10 minutes.
- **Kev** (olive hoodie, the desk on the right). His phone is olive.
  - 3:00: coffee at the kitchen machine.
  - About 3:06: his cable round. He walks across the office to the socket by Lin's desk, crouches and checks her plug
    (about 3:12). If it is in, all is fine (OK thought). If it is out, he pushes it back in (!, thumbs up).
  - About 3:13: back to the coffee machine (from about 3:19).
  - 3:27: back to his desk.
  - On any phone he holds for 4 minutes, then shrugs and hangs up.
- **You** (red jumper, the middle desk with the red mug).
  - **The switchboard.** The office's old cord switchboard stands in the empty reception nook above your desk. Under
    each jack is a card. One shows a briefcase: the outside line, which you cannot use. The others show Lin's face,
    the boss's face, Kev's face and a mug (the yellow wall phone in the kitchen). Plug into a jack and that phone
    rings: its lamp lights, and the phone sends out ripples in its own colour. The ripples show how far the ring
    carries. The owner comes to answer if he is free and can hear it. If not, the nearest free person who can hear it
    answers. A ring that nobody hears stops after a minute and a half. Whoever answers gets hold music (notes) and
    stays on, staring ahead, until he gives up (the times above). The lamp stays lit while the line is in use.
  - **The cord.** You can crouch at the socket by Lin's desk and pull her phone cord out of the wall (it takes a
    moment). The cord then lies loose on the floor, and her phone is dead. You cannot do this while her phone is
    ringing or in use.
  - Both are sneaky. If the boss sees you at either, you are caught. If Lin or Kev sees you, they shout and point
    (!, finger), and the boss sends you to your desk with 20 minutes of work.
  - Away from your desk, the boss also grows suspicious if he sees you standing about, and sends you back to it with
    20 minutes of work. Pretending to be busy (shift) on the way keeps him off you.

## Ways to win
You win when the 3:30 call fails and you are at your desk at 3:34. Each route has its own end card and route icon.

### 1. Someone else answers (route icon: shrug)
Steps:
1. Between about 3:16 and 3:21, while Lin is at her tea and Kev near the coffee machine, ring Kev's phone. Kev hears
   it from the kitchen and goes to his desk to answer it.
2. At once ring the kitchen phone. Kev is busy, so Lin, the free person nearest it, gets up from her tea and answers
   it. She holds for 10 minutes, past 3:30.
3. Go back to your desk.

At 3:30 Lin's desk phone rings red. Lin is still on hold in the kitchen. The boss starts (!), walks out to her phone
and answers it, puzzled (briefcase, ?). He asks after Lin (her face, ?), shrugs and hangs up. Lin has missed her call.

The facts, and when you see them:
- **Lin waits for a call at her desk phone.** At about 3:01 she lifts her receiver, listens and looks up at the
  clock (shots `start`, `lin-checks-the-clock`).
- **The call comes at 3:30 on the outside line and rings her desk phone.** The card gives the time. On the first loop
  at 3:30 you see the briefcase jack's red lamp light, her phone ring red and Lin answer it, delighted (shot
  `the-call`).
- **The switchboard rings phones.** Up close it lights up to show you can use it: a row of jacks under face cards
  (shot `switchboard`). Plug into one and that phone rings in its owner's colour, its lamp lit (shot
  `kev-phone-rings`).
- **The owner walks over, answers and stays on.** Kev hears his phone at the coffee machine (!), walks to his desk
  and stands there with the receiver and the hold music (shots `kev-phone-rings`, `kev-on-hold`).
- **A ring goes to the nearest free person who hears it.** With Kev on his way to his own phone, Lin answers the
  kitchen phone (shot `lin-takes-the-kitchen-phone`). Ring the kitchen alone and Kev, nearer, takes it, so she is
  free again at 3:30 (the teaching fail).
- **Lin holds on, hoping it is her interviewer.** On the kitchen phone she says briefcase, ? and stays on (shot
  `lin-takes-the-kitchen-phone`). She is still there at 3:30 (shot `the-call-answered`).
- **Lin is away from 3:04 to 3:26.** She has her tea at the kitchen table (shot `lin-at-her-tea`).
- **The boss takes Lin's phone when she is away.** At 3:30 he starts and gets up to answer it (shot
  `the-call-answered`).

### 2. Her line is busy (route icon: notes)
Steps:
1. Between about 3:17 and 3:24, while Lin is away from her desk, ring Lin's phone. Not at about 3:23, when she walks
   past the switchboard on her way back: she would see you and tell on you.
2. Go back to your desk.

The boss hears it (!), walks out of his office to her desk and answers it. He likes the hold music and stays on her
line for 9 minutes. Lin comes back at about 3:26 and finds him on her phone. At 3:30 her interviewer gets a busy
line. Lin glares at the boss (his face, angry).

The facts, and when you see them:
- **The boss answers Lin's phone when she is away, and stays on.** Shot `boss-on-lins-phone`: the boss at her desk
  with her teal handset and the hold music (notes, content), Lin walking back.
- **While Lin sits at her phone, she hangs up at once.** You see her pick up, frown and hang up (shot
  `lin-hangs-up-on-her-own-phone`).
- **Too early, and the boss is off her line again before 3:30.** He stays on for 9 minutes. Ring before about 3:17
  and you see him hang up and walk back to his office before the call comes.
- **A busy line means no call.** At 3:30 the boss is still on her line and Lin glares at him (shot
  `the-call-busy`).

### 3. Her phone is dead (route icon: plug)
Steps:
1. At about 3:12, once Kev has checked the socket (he leaves it at about 3:13), ring the boss's phone. He stands at
   his window with his back to the room for 7 minutes.
2. While he is on hold (and Lin is at her tea), go to the socket by Lin's desk and pull her cord out.
3. Go back to your desk.

At 3:30 nothing rings. Lin sits at her silent phone and wonders why her interviewer is late (clock, ?).

A variant: keep Kev from his round. Ring Kev's phone at about 3:02. He is on hold at his desk until about 3:10, runs
out of time for his round and never gets to the socket. Ring the boss too, and unplug once Lin has left at 3:04.

The facts, and when you see them:
- **Her phone needs its cord.** From the start you see the teal cord run from her phone to a socket in the wall
  beside her desk (shot `start`). After you pull it, the cord lies on the floor and the socket is empty (shot
  `cord-pulled`).
- **The boss watches the socket.** His sight cone goes through the glass over Lin's desk and the socket (shot
  `start`). If you pull the cord while he looks, he points at you (shot `end-caught`).
- **On his own phone the boss turns his back.** He stands at the window with the receiver, his sight cone pointing
  out of the window (shot `boss-on-hold`).
- **Kev checks that socket at about 3:12.** Shot `kev-at-the-socket`: Kev crouches at it, OK thought. If the cord is
  out, he pushes it back in (shot `kev-plugs-it-back`).
- **A dead phone means no call.** At 3:30 Lin waits at her silent phone with its cord on the floor (shot
  `the-call-dead`).

### 4. Nobody answers (route icon: bell)
Steps:
1. At about 3:18 ring Kev's phone, then the kitchen phone, as in route 1. Lin is on hold in the kitchen past 3:30.
2. Between about 3:25 and 3:29, ring the boss's phone. He is on hold at his window until after 3:32.
3. Go back to your desk.

At 3:30 Lin's phone rings red at her empty desk. Lin is on hold in the kitchen and the boss at his window. Kev is back
at his desk across the office, too far away to hear it. It rings for two minutes and stops. The end card rounds this
off: it shows all three on hold, though Kev's hold ended at about 3:26.

The facts, and when you see them:
- Everything in route 1, plus: **the boss is the one who answers Lin's phone at 3:30**, and he cannot while he is on
  his own phone (shots `the-call-answered`, `boss-on-hold`).
- **He is on his own phone for 7 minutes.** Ring him at 3:25 and he is still on when the ringing stops at 3:32. Ring
  him earlier and he hangs up in time to answer it (route 1 again). Ring him alone at 3:25 and Lin is back at her
  desk to take her call (the teaching fail).
- **Nobody comes.** Shot `the-call-rings-out`: the red ripples at her empty desk, Lin and the boss on hold elsewhere,
  Kev at his far desk.

## Ways to lose
- **Lin takes her call.** The end card: Lin in your chair, at your desk with your red mug, on the phone, smug
  (briefcase, smile). You stand by with your things in a box, a plant and your nameplate (shot `end-fail`).
- **Caught.** The boss sees you at the socket. The end card: you at the wall with Lin's plug in your fist, its cord
  running back to her phone, and the boss pointing at you, angry (shot `end-caught`).
- **Away from your desk.** The call failed, but at 3:34 your chair is empty. The end card: the boss points at your
  empty chair and desk with your red mug, while you hurry in late (shot `end-blamed`).
- **Told on.** Not a loss by itself. Lin or Kev sees you at the switchboard or the socket and points. The boss sends
  you to your desk with 20 minutes of work, and usually the window has closed when you are free.
- The teaching fails: each picture shows why. Ring only the kitchen and Kev answers it (Lin is free at 3:30). Ring
  Lin's phone while she sits at it and she hangs up. Pull the cord before Kev's round and he plugs it back in. Ring
  only the boss and Lin is at her desk at 3:30. Each ends with Lin taking her call.

## The joke
3:26 to 3:30 on the busy route. Lin hurries back from her tea for the call that will get her your job, and finds the
boss at her desk with her phone at his ear, eyes half shut, enjoying the hold music. He does not budge. At 3:30 her
interviewer gets a busy line, and Lin, beside him, glares at her own boss.
