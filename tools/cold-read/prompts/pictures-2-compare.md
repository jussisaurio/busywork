Read reading.md: a first-time player's reading of a set of screenshots. Read intents.md: what each screenshot must
tell that player. Look at the images in shots/ yourself only to settle a disagreement.

For each shot, mark it `reads` (the reading matches the intent), `partly` or `misread`. For each partly or misread
shot, quote the reading, state the intent, and name the visual cause: an emoji standing in for a person, a glyph
used as a prop, a cause and effect too far apart, a missing cue. Suggest one concrete visual fix.

Each line of intents.md also gives the game clock the shot was taken at, from the engine. The intent is the builder's
words; the clock is when the picture was really taken. When the intent describes another moment (another time, a
beat that is over by then, a moment before the end card the picture shows), mark the shot `wrong moment` instead:
the fix is the shot's commands, not the picture.

Reply with the report. Your reply is saved as report.md.

  - `broken`: the player would not understand the level from these pictures.
- Then the list of shots.
- Then the problems, graded as below. A shot that matters to understanding a route and is misread or taken at the
  wrong moment is a blocker.
