Read reading.md: a first-time player's account of several loops of one level (runs), from pictures alone. Read
runs.md: what each run really was. Its name gives the player's plan and the real cause of the ending. Read ways.md:
the designer's ways to win and lose. Look at the pictures in runs/ yourself only to settle a disagreement.

For each run, mark it:
- `explained`: the reader named the real cause of the ending and the player's action behind it
- `partly`: the reader got the ending but not its cause, or the cause but not the player's part in it
- `unexplained`: the reader could not say why the loop ended, or named the wrong cause
Then list every beat that misled the reader: something they took as a reaction to the player that was not, or a
real reaction they missed. For each partly or unexplained run and each misleading beat, quote the reading, state
what really happened, and name the visual cause: a cause and effect too far apart, a bubble that reads as a
reaction, a missing cue, an emoji standing in for a person. Suggest one concrete visual fix.

Reply with the report. Your reply is saved as report.md.
- The first line is exactly `Verdict: works`, `Verdict: fixable` or `Verdict: broken`.
  - `broken`: the reader can't explain why the player won a run, or can't explain most of the losses. A run where
    the reader can't explain why the player won is `broken`, however pretty the pictures are.
- Then the list of runs.
- Then the problems, graded as below. An unexplained loss, a partly explained win, and a beat that misled the reader
  about a cause are blockers.
