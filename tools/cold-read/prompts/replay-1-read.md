Read card.txt (the level's name and mission card). Then, for each folder in runs/, in order, read its captions.md and
look at its pictures in order. Each folder is one loop of play, recorded automatically: a picture whenever the player
finished an action, someone's bubble came up, or a sound was made, and the end-of-loop picture last. The captions
give the clock time. "You used the thing here" marks where the player did something, but not what: work that out
from the pictures, as a player would. Each run was recorded on its own, as if it were the first loop, so the loop
counter shows 1 in every run.

For each run, write:
- what happened, beat by beat: one line per beat, with the clock time
- why the loop ended in a win or a loss
- which of the player's actions caused that ending
- anything that looked like a reaction to the player but might not be: a bubble, a look, a walk that could have
  another cause
Name things by what they look like, not by what you guess the designer meant. If you can't tell why a loop ended,
say so plainly. Do not invent a cause to fill the gap. Reply with all of it, under 200 words per run. Your reply is
saved as reading.md.
